# Arithmetic Expression Geometry III — Singular Zero Geometry and Tubes

This directory records the destination of singular, multi-zero, tube, braid, and knot
material removed from Paper I.  It is not yet a completed manuscript.

## Imports from Paper I

- the regular and singular AES definitions;
- the regular-zero theorem;
- the smooth total-zero-set and projection-submersion proposition;
- the properness warning.

## Migrated source families

- multi-zero, `E_k`, `E_log`, and tube notes;
- tube-related parts of legacy `sections/sec04.tex` and `sec09.tex`;
- `knots/` and knot-related files under `misc/`;
- restructuring discussions on tubes, braid monodromy, and Markov invariance.

## Status boundary

A global multi-zero construction, singularity normal forms, properness of explicit
families, braid-group monodromy, threading, Markov normalization, and a knot invariant
remain open or structural proposals.  Visual tube constructions do not establish
isotopy or Markov invariance.
