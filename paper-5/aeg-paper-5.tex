\begin{abstract}
Papers~0--IV of this series develop one process-to-result architecture: expansion and
motion, sequential histories and projective semantics, hyperbolic real function
theory, singular zero geometry, and projective condensation with computational cost.
This paper develops a second body of mathematics that those papers do not own: a
theory of three coordinated typed readings of one carrier, of the observation fibres
and residuals such readings produce, and of the syntax of apertures through which
they are filled and checked.

Part~I concerns triadic characteristics.  A finite \emph{observer contract} fixes an
observation map, an equivalence, projectors, and a budget.  A \emph{characteristic}
is then not a value but an operational quotient together with a residual, and the
paper's recurring theorem-shape is negative: values, gradients, spectra, terminal
cycles, frontier snapshots, objectified surreal numbers, and energies do not recover
construction history, and each such failure is stated as a no-go result with the
class of observations fixed.  Within the quadratic scalar family
$A_\kappa=\R[j]/(j^2-\kappa)$ all members satisfy rank-one characteristic recovery,
conjugation, and a multiplicative quadratic norm; a global negative-inverse
involution on the punctured carrier, or equivalently an oriented order-four exchange
lift with $J^2=-I$ while the projective class satisfies $[J]^2=[I]$, selects the
complex member.  \emph{Chirality} is exactly the central sign that projectivization
forgets.  On a declared finite cut interval the six-state carrier $V\oplus\Lambda^2V$
and its orientation expression $\Omega$ with $\Omega^2=-\varepsilon_T\varepsilon_S
\varepsilon_R$ are not postulated: cut-membership cochains together with oriented
interchange generate the Clifford anticommutator, whereas interchange alone provably
cannot.

Three typed interpretations of one finite characteristic algebra --- read temporally,
spatially, and constructively --- are shown to carry a stable variance signature, and
inference is the constrained intersection of the corresponding observation fibres.
The four numeric invariants that a naive theory conflates, namely algebraic degree,
real fibre cardinality, sheet count, and constructive occurrence multiplicity, are
exactly separated on $x\mapsto x^3$; invariance appears as the trivial case of
equivariance; and translation is proved unable to cross a power inside any affine
word normal form, which is why word rewriting must be replaced by typed graph
rewriting with explicit copy and merge.  Classical elliptic isogenies calibrate the
triadic bridge, and the honest conclusion is that the bridge is prior art, so the
programme's claimable novelty is observer relativity and accountable residual.  In
the Legendre family one cusp is simultaneously a branch-factor collision, a vanishing
class carrying a Dehn transvection, and a period monodromy; writing through-crossing
as a cospan with a resolution torsor rather than as a transport arrow derives
$M_{\mathrm{Legendre}}=T_\delta^2$ from the quadratic base change and realizes the
Picard--Lefschetz transvection on a finite cellular annulus.  A Sierpinski-valued
satisfaction form generates a proposition language whose entailment is refinement of
opposite-pair sections, with the standing constraint that the sections, not the
ambient powerset, are the atoms.  The paper reports its own principal negative
calibration: on the finite Legendre prism the total defect decomposes, so no
irreducible triadic $3$-cocycle is exhibited.

Part~II concerns apertures.  The typed hole is taken as the primitive: syntactically
empty, semantically a filling family, and with open and close operations that are
deliberately not inverse.  Over a finite terminal-world carrier, threaded
propositional and predicate logic is proved adequate for its fibre-filling semantics
by structural induction under explicit Cartesian, compatibility, transformer, and
exhaustiveness hypotheses.  An ordered linear natural-deduction fragment is then
proved sound but not complete for Boolean semantics, separated by a grade invariant;
an ordered one-hole substitution theorem gives a ledger splice law, conditional fresh
cut admissibility, and the failure of raw ledger equality; and beta-ledger transport
is shown to lift through every proof context with two decreasing measures, giving
strong normalization.  The critical-pair analysis yields local and global confluence
and a unique normal form per fixed proof, and the resulting normal and neutral
characterization with the subformula property yields a terminating, sound, and
complete focused decision procedure for derivability.  Finally the six-port
whole-cut kernel answers ``why six ports'' by the counting theorem $n\ge 2m$, hence
$n_{\min}=6$, under a stated contract; universality is weakened to relative
initiality in a category of presented models, and the boundary object $M_6^{\partial}$
is shown to be presented by two fixed-point-free involutions whose union is connected.

The two parts are joined by one discipline.  A quotient is admissible only with a
declared residual; a calibration is never promoted to a theorem; and triadicity,
universality, and periodicity are properties to be certified rather than names to be
assumed.  The paper closes with an explicit list of what it does not establish.
\end{abstract}
