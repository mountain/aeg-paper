## An open research project

AEG is an **open research project**, and this paper is offered in that spirit: we are
seeking collaborating researchers.  You do not need to accept the whole architecture in
order to contribute here, because the paper states its own boundaries explicitly and the
unfinished places are the intended entry points.  The four gaps listed under *Known gaps*
below are the most useful ones to attack, and §17 gives their dependence order: order
comes first, then the filler, then triadicity, then motion.

Refutations are as welcome as extensions.  If a calibration is wrong, if a hypothesis is
too strong, or if a claim's inherited status overstates what the source note established,
that is a contribution and not a setback.  Please open an issue or a pull request against
this repository, naming the claim you touch and whether your change strengthens, weakens,
or refutes it.

## What the paper contains

The paper is a two-part monograph.

**Part I — Triadic characteristic geometry.**  An observer contract, observation fibres, and
the characteristic-as-quotient-plus-residual principle; the quadratic scalar trichotomy and
the isolation of chirality as a retained central sign; the six-state carrier
$V\oplus\Lambda^2V$ with the Clifford relation derived from cut data; triadic characteristic
inference and its variance signature; typed multiplicity and the word-to-graph boundary;
classical calibrations by elliptic isogenies and the Legendre crossing; through-crossings as
cospans with resolution torsors, and monodromy as a difference of resolutions; a logic
generated by a Sierpinski-valued satisfaction form; and a catalogue of shadows that fail to
determine construction.

**Part II — Threaded apertures and the six-port kernel.**  The typed hole as primitive;
finite adequacy of threaded logic under four named hypotheses; ordered linear natural
deduction with soundness and provable incompleteness; ordered substitution with an exact
ledger splice; strong normalization, confluence, and decidable derivability; and the
six-port whole-cut kernel with the counting theorem $n\ge 2m$, hence $n_{\min}=6$, together
with the $M_6$ boundary bridge.

The two parts meet at the filling fibre: in Part I a residual is what a quotient cannot see,
and in Part II an aperture is where a partial reading records that it cannot yet see.

## Claim discipline

The paper uses the repository's status vocabulary with three dedicated environments:

* `\begin{calibration}` — established only by a finite exhaustive check of a declared
  fixture;
* `\begin{proposal}` — a definitional or organisational choice with no theorem attached;
* `\begin{conjecture}` — explicitly unproved;
* `\begin{nogo}` — a proved negative result, which is a theorem and is proved as one.

Three rules govern the whole manuscript.  A quotient is admissible only with a declared
residual.  A calibration is never promoted to a theorem.  A property is certified rather than
named: triadicity, universality, and periodicity are all properties to be proved, not labels
to be applied.

papers 0--5.

## Known gaps

Recorded in §17, and repeated here so they are not lost:

1. **Order from energy** — nothing in this material derives a comparison from period-energy
   data; this is the decisive gap on which the triadic programme depends.
2. **Non-decomposable triadic defect** — the finite Legendre prism and the finite
   satisfaction form both fail to exhibit one.
3. **The $M_6$ filler** — the boundary is presented; the 2-cell is not constructed.
4. **A typed feedback operator** — boundary-return words exist, periodic dynamics does not.
