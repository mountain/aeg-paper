## Mathematical objects

### E0 upper half-plane, grid generators, grid words
- source: 0032 (with 0033, 0034)
- definition: $\mathfrak E_0=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:y>0\}$ with assignment $a(x,y)=-x/y$; grid moves $X_s(x,y)=(x-sy,y)$ and $Y_k(x,y)=(x,y/k)$, so $a\circ X_s=a+s$, $a\circ Y_k=ka$. A grid word is a composite of $X_s^{\pm1},Y_k^{\pm1}$; it is an *oriented one-variable affine routing model*, i.e. generators act by $t\mapsto t+s$ and $t\mapsto kt$, $k>0$.

### Elementary E0 relation (Baumslag–Solitar)
- source: 0032
- definition: For $m\ge2$, $Y_m^{-1}X_s^mY_m=X_s$; concretely $Y^{-1}X^2Y=X$ with $X=X_1$, $Y=Y_2$, the $\mathrm{BS}(2,1)$ relation, realized as the boundary word of one planar cell. Conjugating by $J$ gives the reversed-scale polarity form $B^{-1}X^\vee B=(X^\vee)^2$ with $X^\vee=JXJ$, $Y^\vee=JYJ$, $B=(Y^\vee)^{-1}$, i.e. $\mathrm{BS}(1,2)$.

### Routing embedding of the checked diamond
- source: 0032
- definition: The program `copy -> {neg,id} -> add` is assigned exact rational E0 points (input $(0,2)$, copy $(0,1)$, neg $(-1,1)$, id $(0,1/2)$, branch midpoint $(-1/2,1/2)$, add $(-1,1/2)$, output $(-1,1/4)$) with assignments $a=0,0,1,0,1,2,4$; the six wires are routed by the words $Y$, $X$, $Y$, $Y$, $XX$, $Y$. The inner cell boundary is $(0,1)\xrightarrow{Y}(0,\tfrac12)\xrightarrow{X}(-\tfrac12,\tfrac12)\xrightarrow{X}(-1,\tfrac12)\xrightarrow{Y^{-1}}(-1,1)\xrightarrow{X^{-1}}(0,1)$.

### Möbius involution $J$, horocycle and geodesic-circle families
- source: 0032 (used in 0033, 0034)
- definition: $J(z)=-1/z$, i.e. $J(x,y)=\bigl(-x/(x^2+y^2),\;y/(x^2+y^2)\bigr)$ on $\mathfrak E_0$; $a(Jp)=-a(p)$ (polarity reversal). $J$ maps $y=c$ to the horocycle $u^2+(v-\tfrac1{2c})^2=\tfrac1{4c^2}$ tangent to the real axis at $0$, maps $x=d\ne0$ to the circle $(u+\tfrac1{2d})^2+v^2=\tfrac1{4d^2}$ orthogonal to the real axis, and preserves $x=0$. $J(G)$ is thus a *curved* image grid, not a relabelling.

### Two duals and the wire-indexed edge bridge
- source: 0032
- definition: $J(G)$ (Möbius image of the E0 grid) and $G^\dagger$ (cellular dual of the planar program embedding) are kept distinct; the checked wire set $E_{\mathrm{wire}}$ supplies the common finite index $J(G)\xleftarrow{J\circ\iota}E_{\mathrm{wire}}\xrightarrow{\star}G^\dagger$, and on wire-indexed mod-two one-chains the bridge is $\delta_G(J(e))=e^\dagger$.

### Prefix-free halting mass $\Omega_U$
- source: 0033 (also 0036, 0037)
- definition: For a prefix-free partial $U:\{0,1\}^*\rightharpoonup X$ with fair-coin cylinders $\mu([p])=2^{-|p|}$, $\Omega_U=\mu\bigl(\bigcup_{U(p)\downarrow}[p]\bigr)=\sum_{U(p)\downarrow}2^{-|p|}\le1$ (Kraft bound); for universal $U$ it is left-c.e., noncomputable and Martin-Löf random, and machine-dependent — hence "a chart", not a coordinate-free invariant.

### Two-index dyadic approximation ($\Omega$ bifiltration)
- source: 0033
- definition: $\Omega_{n,t}=\sum_{|p|\le n,\;U(p)\text{ halts in }t\text{ steps}}2^{-|p|}$, computable dyadic, monotone in both indices, with $\Omega_U=\sup_{n,t}\Omega_{n,t}$; $n$ = prefix/birthday depth, $t$ = causal/runtime depth. The pair is a *bifiltration, not yet a double period*; universality removes any computable modulus of convergence.

### Effective cut $(L_\Omega\mid R_\Omega)$
- source: 0033
- definition: $L_\Omega=\{r\in\mathbb Q:r<\Omega_U\}$, $R_\Omega=\{r\in\mathbb Q:r>\Omega_U\}$; $L_\Omega$ is c.e. (dovetail halts, add weights), $R_\Omega$ is not c.e. for universal $U$. A computational $L\mid R$ therefore exposes an unresolved boundary or needs an oracle; it is not an ordinary completed finite cut.

### Objectification fibre mass
- source: 0033

### Mod-two slope classes ($\mathbf P^1(\mathbb F_2)$)
- source: 0034 (inherited by 0037)
- definition: $\delta_T=(1,0)$, $\delta_S=(0,1)$, $\delta_C=(1,1)$, i.e. exactly the three points of $\mathbf P^1(\mathbb F_2)$, with cyclic identities $\delta_T=\delta_S+\delta_C$, $\delta_S=\delta_C+\delta_T$, $\delta_C=\delta_T+\delta_S$ over $\mathbb F_2$. The classes are the reductions mod 2 of primitive slopes $(p,q)$; since $\Gamma(2)$ acts trivially mod 2 they cannot be identified inside $Y(2)$.

### Oriented integer lift of the three cycles
- source: 0037
- definition: $\delta_T=a$, $\delta_S=b$, $\delta_C=-a-b$ in $H_1(E_\tau,\mathbb Z)$ with $\delta_T+\delta_S+\delta_C=0$ and intersections $\langle\delta_T,\delta_S\rangle=\langle\delta_S,\delta_C\rangle=\langle\delta_C,\delta_T\rangle=1$ (compatible orientation). For $h=xa+yb$ the three local readings are $(p_C,q_C)=(x,y)$, $(p_T,q_T)=(y-x,-x)$, $(p_S,q_S)=(-y,x-y)$, and the order-three matrix $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$ ($R^3=I$) sends $\delta_T\to\delta_S\to\delta_C\to\delta_T$. This lift, not the mod-two picture, carries chirality/sign.

### Level-two modular curve and its three cusps
- source: 0034, 0037

The base is smooth — singularities live in the *fibres over* cusp points, not at base points. The lambda function is the quotient coordinate.

## Results

### Effective-cut one-sidedness
- source: 0033
- kind: proposition
- status: established; proof given inline (dovetail argument), cited to Chaitin/Barmpalias
- statement: For a universal prefix-free $U$, $L_\Omega=\{r\in\mathbb Q:r<\Omega_U\}$ is c.e. but $R_\Omega=\{r\in\mathbb Q:r>\Omega_U\}$ is not c.e.; were both enumerable, effective rational brackets would make $\Omega_U$ computable. Hence the left side is generable by finite positive evidence while the right side requires nonhalting information, and a computational $L\mid R$ cut is intrinsically asymmetric.
- engineering dependency: none

### Kraft bound and cylinder measure of halting
- source: 0033
- kind: proposition
- status: established standard result
- statement: If $\mathrm{dom}(U)$ is prefix-free, the cylinders $[p]$ with $U(p)\downarrow$ are pairwise disjoint, so $\Omega_U=\sum_{U(p)\downarrow}2^{-|p|}=\mu\bigl(\bigcup[p]\bigr)\le1$; surreally $\Omega_U$ is a genuine probability with exact dyadic finite approximations, and its value depends on $U$ and the grammar (machine as chart).
- engineering dependency: none

### Monotone two-index approximation of $\Omega_U$
- source: 0033
- kind: proposition
- status: elementary; implemented design target (not a theorem about Adva)
- statement: $\Omega_{n,t}$ is a computable dyadic rational, monotone nondecreasing in $n$ and in $t$, and $\Omega_U=\sup_{n,t}\Omega_{n,t}$; universality admits no computable modulus telling the machine when the 2-D approximation is close. The pair $(n,t)$ is a bifiltration (description depth $\times$ runtime), and it is *not* yet a double period.
- engineering dependency: none

### Objectification fibre mass is a semimeasure that does not repair the retraction obstruction
- source: 0033
- kind: proposition
- status: definition plus elementary verification; explicitly denies a repair of the 0021 no-go
- statement: $m_{U,Q}(x)=\sum\{2^{-|p|}:U(p)\downarrow,\ \mathrm{Obj}_Q(U(p))=x\}$ is a lower-semicomputable semimeasure quantifying how much encoded intensional history the fibre $\mathrm{Obj}_Q^{-1}(x)$ collapsed (e.g. $(0\mid2)$ and $(0\mid3)$ objectify to the same surreal while contributing separate mass). A scalar mass cannot recover the members of a fibre, so the decorated form and the residual of the failed objectification square remain mandatory.
- engineering dependency: partial

### The three cusps of $X(2)$ are the three nonzero mod-two slope classes
- source: 0034 (lemmas restated 0037 §2.2)
- kind: proposition
- status: exact external standard fact (level-two modular geometry); "not numerology"
- statement: $Y(2)=\Gamma(2)\backslash\mathbb H\cong\mathbf P^1\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}$ has exactly three cusps $\infty,0,1$, and for a primitive slope $(p,q)$ reduction mod 2 yields exactly three nonzero parity classes; since $\Gamma(2)$ acts trivially mod 2 these classes cannot be identified inside $Y(2)$. Three semantic aspects therefore need not be three independent dimensions but can be the three nonzero polarities of one rank-two marked state.
- engineering dependency: none

### Cyclic opposite-edge identities over $\mathbb F_2$
- source: 0034
- kind: lemma
- status: trivial after the marking; "exact, not an analogy"
- statement: $\delta_T=\delta_S+\delta_C$, $\delta_S=\delta_C+\delta_T$, $\delta_C=\delta_T+\delta_S$ over $\mathbb F_2$, with pairwise intersection determinants of absolute value one, so the three directions form a minimal Farey triangle rather than three coordinate axes. In particular $\delta_C=\delta_T+\delta_S$ reads construction as the diagonal produced when a temporal history closes against a spatial cut.
- engineering dependency: none

### Tri-chart coordinate coherence (Lemma A)
- source: 0037

- engineering dependency: none

## Threads
- **Intrinsic obstruction by residual.** The batch's most reliable results are negative or boundary results: objectification collapses distinct decorated forms (0021, used in 0033/0035/0037), endpoint cycles do not determine the trace (0035), mod-two erases chirality (0034/0037), E0 routing is not a valuation (0032), energy is not order (0037), local cusp monodromy is spectrally trivial (0034). In every case the correction is the same: keep a decorated carrier plus an explicit, auditable residual, and let shadows (numbers, cycles, eigenvalues, spectra) be partial quotients.
- **The triangle as a rank-two carrier read three ways.** Time/space/construction start as semantic labels (0034), become the three nonzero classes of $\mathbf P^1(\mathbb F_2)$ and then of an oriented period lattice with exact order-three transitions (0037), become three typed grammars and computers (0036), and are provisionally identified with three local split/merge/handle roles (0035). The recurring warning: three names are not a theory until the labelling is canonical under chart rotation and carried by operational data.
- **Effective one-sidedness of completion.** Kraft measure $\to$ c.e. left side / non-c.e. right side $\to$ `Ω`-type boundary (0033) $\to$ conditional no-certifiable-omniscience for learners (0036,0037). The valuable invariant is the *type* of boundary (left-c.e., noncomputable, random, completable only as a directed supremum), never the machine's number.
- **From static chains to thickened traces.** The checked one-step surgery law integrates to a relative face chain (0035); a chain is not a surface, so the programme asks for a time-thickened decorated trace, then a finite cellulation, then a state functor, then a handle operator, then a spectrum — each arrow separately falsifiable, with the no-hidden-genus obligation at the join.
- **Finite observation as the generation layer.** Features appear only after controlled forgetting: observational quotients, two-channel forgetting with residual, descended dynamics (0036), specialized into the surreal reduction contract with budget $B=(n,\Lambda,K,\varepsilon)$ (0037). Compression without a task is not a feature; a global observer or global section may fail to exist and that failure is a legitimate result.
- **Order versus scale.** Energy/period shells select *what is visible*; the Conway $L\mid R$ order is a different structure that no argument in the batch constructs. This is the single acknowledged decisive gap (0037 §3.5), and it is the point where every earlier layer (period grade, spectral packet, cut) fails to close.
- **Discipline about what a checked program buys.** Calibration is never promoted: exhaustive fixture checks (0032 star-square, 0035 pairs/triples, 0037 unit shell) are labelled as finite conditional evidence, and each note pre-registers its own falsification criteria.
