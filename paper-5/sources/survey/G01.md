## Threads
- **Raising the ontology before linearizing.** The carrier is progressively moved from matrices (0001) to native affine programs (0002), to program cells and cut lattices (0004), to causal-open topologies with decorated frontiers (0005–0007). Matrices, polynomial normal forms and spectra recur only as compiled shadows.
- **Space–time duality read from one checked diagram.** Ordered traces vs Boolean cut-refinement (0004), causal poset vs Alexandrov opens (0005), generation edges vs cut cochains (0010), and backward section transport vs forward evaluation (0017) are all two readings of one object rather than two structures glued together.
- **The occurrence/expression carrier and its derived shadows.** Occurrence-decorated expression sections (0007, 0015–0019) are claimed as the native pre-spectral object; polynomial-like and matrix-like presentations are co-generated observations, and the matrix shadow equals the Jacobian transpose only after a port chart is declared.
- **A systematic no-go pattern: extensional equality never recovers construction.** Equal values, derivatives, spectra, gradients, terminal cycles, frontier snapshots, or objectified surreal numbers repeatedly fail to determine history, source occurrence, schedule, or scope (0001, 0002, 0004, 0006, 0012, 0015, 0020, 0021, 0023, 0025, 0027, 0030).
- **Chirality as a retained central sign.** The projective involution $z\mapsto-1/z$ forgets the sign that the oriented lift keeps; $J^2=-I$ vs $[J]^2=1$, $\Omega$ vs $-\Omega$, and forward vs inverse device histories are the same phenomenon at four degrees of abstraction (0008–0013, 0020, 0029).
- **Field selection is deferred and made conditional.** Complex, dual and split scalars are all compatible with the six-degree-of-freedom bookkeeping; only the negative-inverse involution, or the order-four exchange lift under a bridge hypothesis, selects $\mathbb C$, and even then the derivation is not from program geometry (0007, 0008, 0009).
- **The $6=3+3=2+2+2$ carrier from causality itself.** Wedge from oriented interchange plus contraction from cut membership yields the Clifford relation and the order-four orientation action endogenously on a local Boolean cut interval (0009, 0010), with a derived filtration $1+2+2+1$ depending on a chosen causal line (0011).
- **Physical closure as calibration, not derivation.** Paraxial optics reproduces the order-four lift and, via one explicit surjective closure, commutes with $\Omega$; but the bridge is a declared chart and the kernel is not certified physically irrelevant (0012–0014).
- **The $PSP^*$ programme and its successive weakening.** Cut-level star-factorization (0020) requires surgery on decorated dual cycles; objectification no-go (0021) forbids quotienting before analysis; relational polarization, nesting and decoration (0022–0031) progressively replace one-$P$ conjugation with boundary-polarized relations and layered decorations.
