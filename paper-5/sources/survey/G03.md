## Mathematical objects

### Observer contract $Q$
- source: 0038 (used in all five)
- definition: A quadruple $Q=(\mathcal F_Q,\equiv_Q,\Pi_Q,B_Q)$: bounded candidate characteristic space, declared observation relation, finite family of probes/boundaries, finite resource budget. Every uniqueness statement in the batch is relative to such a $Q$.

### Local, cumulative, stationary characteristic
- source: 0038
- definition: A local factor explains one adjacent transition $b_{i-1}\xrightarrow{\lambda_i}b_i$; the cumulative characteristic is the ordered fold $\chi_n=\lambda_n\odot\cdots\odot\lambda_1$; the stationary characteristic is the case $\lambda_1=\cdots=\lambda_n=c$.

### Characteristic algebra $(\mathcal F_Q,\odot,1)$
- source: 0038
- definition: A finite or finitely bounded monoid of characteristic factors with a chronological product $f_2\odot f_1$ (execute $f_1$ first).

### Typed interpretations $\rho_t,\rho_X,\rho_K$
- source: 0038
- definition: $\rho_t:\mathcal F_Q\to\operatorname{End}(T_Q)$, $\rho_X:\mathcal F_Q^{\mathrm{op}}\to\operatorname{End}(\mathsf{Open}(X_Q))$ (inverse image, hence contravariant), $\rho_K:\mathcal F_Q\to\operatorname{End}(K_{\mathrm{can},Q})$ (canonical construction forms).

### Observation fibre and characteristic set
- source: 0038
- definition: $\operatorname{Fib}_{D,Q}(o_D)=\{f\in\mathcal F_Q:\rho_D(f)\equiv_Q o_D\}$ and $\operatorname{Char}_Q(o_t,o_X,o_K)=\operatorname{Fib}_{t,Q}(o_t)\cap\operatorname{Fib}_{X,Q}(o_X)\cap\operatorname{Fib}_{K,Q}(o_K)$.

### Raw history, canonical section, residual
- source: 0038
- definition: $K_{\mathrm{raw}}\xrightarrow{\chi_K}\mathcal F_Q\xrightarrow{\operatorname{NF}}K_{\mathrm{can}}$ with $\chi_K\circ\operatorname{NF}=\operatorname{id}_{\mathcal F_Q}$ but $\operatorname{NF}\circ\chi_K\ne\operatorname{id}_{K_{\mathrm{raw}}}$; a result is a triple $(\chi_K(p),\pi_p,R_{K,Q}(p))$ of characteristic, checked reduction witness, and intensional residual.

### Binary affine characteristic monoid
- source: 0038
- definition: $\mathcal F_{\mathrm{aff}}=\mathbf N\times\mathbf N$ with $\chi(S)=(0,1)$, $\chi(D)=(1,0)$, product $f_2\odot f_1=(k_1+k_2,\,2^{k_2}b_1+b_2)$, identity $(0,0)$, denotation $2^kx+b$.

### Interval convention $I(m,r)$
- source: 0038
- definition: $I(m,r)=(m-r,m+r)$, $r>0$; $\rho_X(k,b)(I(m,r))=f_{k,b}^{-1}(I(m,r))=I\!\left((m-b)/2^k,\ r/2^k\right)$.

### Bounded triadic characteristic form
- source: 0038
- definition: $\mathfrak C_Q=(\mathcal F_Q,\odot,1;\rho_t,\rho_X,\rho_K;\chi_K,\operatorname{NF};\omega_{\mathrm{eval}},\omega_{\mathrm{open}};R_Q)$, where $\omega_{\mathrm{eval}}$ is $\operatorname{eval}\circ\rho_K(f)=\rho_t(f)\circ\operatorname{eval}$ and $\omega_{\mathrm{open}}$ is $x\in\rho_X(f)(U)\Leftrightarrow\rho_t(f)(x)\in U$.

### Square map and sign quotient
- source: 0039
- definition: $q(x)=x^2$, $\sigma(x)=-x$, $q\circ\sigma=q$; $x\sim_q x'$ iff $x'=\pm x$; $\mathbf R\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathbf R/C_2\xrightarrow{\cong}[0,\infty)\hookrightarrow\mathbf R$, with $C_2=\{1,\sigma\}$ acting non-freely at the origin.

### Reverse fibre $q^\dagger$
- source: 0039
- definition: $q^\dagger(y)=\{x:x^2=y\}$, equal to $\varnothing$ for $y<0$, $\{0\}$ for $y=0$, $\{-\sqrt y,\sqrt y\}$ for $y>0$; a relation, not a function.

### Graph correspondence $\Gamma_q$ with projections $s,t$
- source: 0039
- definition: $\Gamma_q=\{(x,y):y=x^2\}$ with $s(x,y)=x$, $t(x,y)=y$; then $x\mapsto t(s^{-1}(x))$ is forward evaluation, $q^{-1}(U)=s(t^{-1}(U))$ is spatial inverse image, $q^\dagger(y)=s(t^{-1}(y))$ is the reverse fibre, and $\widetilde\sigma(x,y)=(-x,y)$ satisfies $t\circ\widetilde\sigma=t$.

### Critical source set and critical value set
- source: 0039 (typed in 0040)
- definition: $R_q=\{x:q'(x)=0\}\subseteq$ source, $B_q=q(R_q)\subseteq$ target; for $x^2$ both are $\{0\}$ but they are different typed objects.

### Fan-out and merge $\Delta_n,\mu_n$
- source: 0039, 0040
- definition: $x\xrightarrow{\Delta_n}(x_0,\dots,x_{n-1})\xrightarrow{\mu_n}\prod_i x_i$; for $x^2$, $\Delta_2$ then multiplication; for $x^3$, a two-stage copy tree giving three leaves with distinct occurrence identity and one common source.

### Layered square characteristic $\widehat Q$
- source: 0039
- definition: $\widehat Q=(\Gamma_q,s,t;\ \sigma;\ R_q,B_q;\ \kappa_q)$: extensional graph, branch-exchange involution, typed critical sets, and a checked constructive copy/multiply witness; packaged further as $\mathfrak C_{\square,Q}=(\mathcal G_Q;\rho_t,\rho_X,\rho_K;\Sigma_Q;\operatorname{Crit}_s,\operatorname{Crit}_t;\chi_K,\pi_K,R_K)$.

### Quadratic hypothesis class
- source: 0039
- definition: $\mathcal F_2=\{f_{a,b,c}(x)=ax^2+bx+c\}$ with coefficients in a declared finite budget; probes at $-1,0,1$ give $c=y_0$, $a=(y_1+y_{-1}-2y_0)/2$, $b=(y_1-y_{-1})/2$.

### Branch involution on open sets $\sigma_X$
- source: 0039

### Observer-relative degree profile $\mathbf d_Q(P)$
- source: 0040
- definition: $\mathbf d_Q(P)=(d_{\mathrm{alg}},d_{\mathrm{fib},Q},d_{\mathrm{sheet},Q},d_{\mathrm{occ},Q})$: algebraic degree, generic reverse-fibre cardinality, local spatial sheet count, and constructive occurrence multiplicity under a declared observer. Values: $\mathbf d_{Q_{\mathbf R}}(x^3)=(3,1,1,3)$, $\mathbf d_{Q_{\mathbf C}}(x^3)=(3,3,3,3)$.

### Invariance versus equivariance
- source: 0040
- definition: A characteristic judgment is invariant if $\chi(g\cdot p)=\chi(p)$ and equivariant if $\chi(g\cdot p)=\pi_Q(g)\chi(p)$; the target action $\pi_Q$ is part of the observer contract. For powers $P_n(-x)=(-1)^nP_n(x)$: even $n$ carries the trivial, odd $n$ the sign representation of $C_2$.

### Binomial coaction on $\mathbf Q[x]$ and the translation-closed power vocabulary
- source: 0040
- definition: $\Delta(x)=x\otimes1+1\otimes x$, so $\Delta(x^3)=x^3\otimes1+3x^2\otimes x+3x\otimes x^2+1\otimes x^3$ and $(\operatorname{id}\otimes\operatorname{ev}_b)\Delta(x^3)=(x+b)^3$; the minimal power vocabulary closed under crossing a translation through the cube is at least $\{1,I,Q,C\}$, and in bounded degree $n$ it is $\{1,x,\dots,x^n\}$ with the binomial law.

### Revised cube characteristic $\widehat C_Q$
- source: 0040
- definition: $\widehat C_Q=(\Gamma_C,s,t;\ G_Q,\pi_Q;\ R_C,B_C;\ \kappa_C;\ \mathbf d_Q;\ \mathcal R_Q)$ adding an observer symmetry group, a target transformation law, typed critical data, and an observer-relative degree profile.

### Complex elliptic isogeny with three readings

### Lattice multiplier $M_\alpha$ and kernel
- source: 0041

### Dual isogeny $\widehat\phi$
- source: 0041

### Vélu kernel-to-quotient construction
- source: 0041
- definition: A finite subgroup $G\subset E(\overline k)$ determines a separable quotient $G\mapsto(E/G,\phi_G)$, unique up to isomorphism of the codomain; the rational map is construction data, not determined by the morphism.

### Legendre family $E_\lambda$ and its discriminant
- source: 0042

### Binary quartic branch presentation and cusp words
- source: 0042

### Picard–Fuchs operator
- source: 0042

### Vanishing lattice and $A_2$ pattern
- source: 0042

### Dehn transvection $L_\delta$ and squared cusp action $U_\delta$
- source: 0042

### Crossing data, comparison certificates, coherence defect $\Theta_c$, and vanishing carrier $V_c$
- source: 0042

## Results

### Triadic variance law $(+,-,+)$
- source: 0038 (reaffirmed 0039, 0040)
- kind: proposition
- status: established by hand inside the finite affine model; explicitly not claimed as a theorem of every future three-domain model.
- statement: For $f_1$ executed before $f_2$, $\rho_t(f_2\odot f_1)=\rho_t(f_2)\circ\rho_t(f_1)$, $\rho_X(f_2\odot f_1)=\rho_X(f_1)\circ\rho_X(f_2)$, and $\rho_K(f_2\odot f_1)\equiv_{\mathrm{can}}\rho_K(f_2)\circ\rho_K(f_1)$; schematically $\mathfrak T(f_2\odot f_1)=(T_2\circ T_1,\ X_1\circ X_2,\ K_2\circ K_1\bmod\mathrm{can})$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Characteristic inference is intersection of typed observation fibres
- source: 0038
- kind: proposition
- status: definitional identification; the shared "one algorithm" is a shared operation in characteristic space, not a shared untyped decoder.
- statement: With $\operatorname{Fib}_{D,Q}(o_D)=\{f:\rho_D(f)\equiv_Q o_D\}$, the inferred set is $\operatorname{Fib}_{t,Q}(o_t)\cap\operatorname{Fib}_{X,Q}(o_X)\cap\operatorname{Fib}_{K,Q}(o_K)$; each interpreter contributes constraints and the constraints are intersected.
- engineering dependency: none

### Four-valued inference outcome discipline
- source: 0038
- kind: definition
- status: methodological requirement; an empty candidate set counts as inconsistency only under certified exhaustion.
- statement: Results are `Unique`, `Ambiguous`, `Inconsistent`, or `Unknown`; timeout or incomplete enumeration must return `Unknown` and never a proof of nonexistence.
- engineering dependency: partial

### Binary affine monoid product law
- source: 0038
- kind: lemma
- status: exact algebraic identity, checked by expanding the composite.
- statement: For $f_i(x)=2^{k_i}x+b_i$ executed in order, $f_2\odot f_1=(k_1+k_2,\,2^{k_2}b_1+b_2)$ with identity $(0,0)$; hence $\chi(S;D)=(1,2)\ne(1,1)=\chi(D;S)$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Deformed crossing $S;D\equiv D;S;S$ (order reduction is not a swap)
- source: 0038
- kind: proposition
- status: one exact extensional crossing law; the note explicitly does not claim a terminating, confluent rewriting theorem for the abstract presentation.
- statement: $S;D\equiv D;S;S$ extensionally while $S;D\not\equiv D;S$; moving $D$ leftward across $S$ duplicates the translation generator, and every pair has canonical representative $\operatorname{NF}(k,b)=D^k;S^b$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Two-probe temporal recovery of $(k,b)$
- source: 0038
- kind: proposition
- status: unique only relative to the declared affine hypothesis class; a single probe at $x=1$ cannot separate $S$ from $D$.
- statement: $y_0=\rho_t(k,b)(0)=b$ and $y_1=2^k+b$, so $b=y_0$ and $2^k=y_1-y_0$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Spatial recovery of $(k,b)$ from one exact interval
- source: 0038
- kind: proposition
- status: relies on the unusually informative exact-interval observer; radius alone cannot recover $b$.
- statement: $\rho_X(k,b)(I(m,r))=I((m-b)/2^k,r/2^k)$, so from $I(m,r)\mapsto I(m',r')$ one gets $2^k=r/r'$ and $b=m-2^km'$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Compression of the characteristic as parameters, not expanded word
- source: 0038
- kind: proposition
- status: a narrow representation rule; explicitly not a theorem that every characteristic is shorter than every source program.
- statement: $\chi(S;D^n)=(n,2^n)$ whose canonical word $D^n;S^{2^n}$ has exponentially many $S$ symbols, while the parameter pair needs only $O(n)$ bits; the compression is inseparable from the quotient losing information.
- engineering dependency: none

### Quotient is lossy: $\chi_K\circ\operatorname{NF}=\operatorname{id}$ but $\operatorname{NF}\circ\chi_K\ne\operatorname{id}$
- source: 0038

### Temporal information loss is a $C_2$ orbit quotient
- source: 0039

### No affine crossing for translation through the square (coefficient obstruction)
- source: 0039
- kind: no-go
- status: exact obstruction inside the affine post-normal-form language only; it forbids an affine $A$, not a non-affine expansion.
- statement: There is no affine $A(y)=\alpha y+\beta$ with $S_b;Q\equiv Q;A$ for $b\ne0$, because every post-affine square has the form $\alpha x^2+\beta$ with zero linear coefficient while $(x+b)^2=x^2+2bx+b^2$ has $2b\ne0$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Temporal identifiability is relative to the hypothesis class
- source: 0039 (also 0038, 0040)

### Constructive characteristic is not recoverable from the polynomial

### Exponential gap between circuit and expanded polynomial

## Threads

- **One algebra, three typed interpretations, one intersection solver.** The invariant core across 0038–0042: a single finite characteristic algebra read temporally (covariant), spatially (contravariant, inverse image), and constructively (covariant modulo canonicalisation), with inference as intersection of typed observation fibres.
- **Variance signature $(+,-,+)$ survives every test.** Established in the affine model, and re-verified through non-injectivity (0039), non-quadratic powers (0040), and the classical isogeny bridge (0041, 0042). What changes across the batch is the *structure needed per interpretation*, not the variance law.
- **The carrier must be upgraded repeatedly, never discarded.** Endomorphism monoid (0038) → graph correspondence plus branch symmetry and critical decorations (0039) → equivariance law plus observer-relative degree profile (0040) → typed morphism plus chart/port data (0041) → stratified base with crossing cells and a coherence defect (0042).
- **Deformed crossing, never commutative swap.** $S;D=D;S^2$ (0038), $D_a;Q=Q;D_{a^2}$ (0039), $D_a;C=C;D_{a^3}$ and $N;C=C;N$ (0040), label transport $L_{g\delta}=gL_\delta g^{-1}$ (0042); the persistent obstruction is translation, which expands a word into a typed DAG.
- **Control of information loss: quotient plus residual.** The characteristic is an operational quotient, and every result must carry a certificate and a residual; raw constructive history is never reconstructible from the extensional quotient.
- **Observer-relativity as a first-class object.** Hypothesis class does the identifiability work (0038–0040), scalar domain changes branch count ($\mathbf R$ vs $\mathbf C$ in 0040), and 0042 adds a full refinement tower with a proposed $\Omega$-type boundary.
- **Multiplicity is typed, not scalar.** Temporal orbit size, covering degree, real fibre cardinality, sheet count, and constructive occurrence count are systematically separated and prevented from serving as definitions of one another (0039 §1.6, 0040 §1.2, 0041 §6).
- **Invariance generalised to equivariance.** Sign reversal gives trivial versus sign representation of $C_2$ for even versus odd powers (0040), and this becomes the prototype for how a characteristic may transform rather than merely persist.
- **Local singular data generates global structure.** Atiyah's local-to-global theme, realised finitely as: three cusp operators generate global transport, projective closure forgets a central sign that a chosen lift retains (0042).
- **Positive results are stated as calibrations bounded by explicit non-claims.** Every note ends with a "what is not established" list; the batch's epistemic style is that obstruction and failed unification would themselves be progress.
