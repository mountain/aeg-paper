## Threads
- **Normalization versus transport.** Braid/Coxeter transport reorders a flat boundary but cannot remove containment or an equation defect (0059, 0060, 0061); an active, non-invertible operation is required, and it must stay typed apart from reversible transport.
- **Surface versus fibre.** Every note in the batch insists that a flat or closed visible surface is a quotient: `{}[]()` never means quiescence, provenance-freedom, program equality, or holonomy triviality (0059–0061, 0068, 0069, 0078).
- **Reverse duality, contravariantly.** A single discipline recurs: proof normalizes toward a sink, learning is the reverse generative orientation, duality acts as $\star$ on arrows with $(f:c\to c')^\star:(c')^\star\to c^\star$, and the forward `NF/NF` equation is false (0066, 0067, 0068, 0070, 0078).
- **Holonomy and the circle.** Three typed charts whose overlaps are nonempty while the triple intersection is empty produce $\partial\Delta^2$; gluing is then controlled by fixed points of a circuit permutation, with flat/residual/obstructed as three genuinely different outcomes (0067, 0068, 0070, 0069).
- **Through forms as fibre products over a singular middle.** $\langle L\mid_N R\rangle=F_-\times_N F_+$ is the batch's central new syntax, and the singular node packages multivaluedness into a resolution fibre instead of erasing it (0070, 0075, 0071, 0072).
- **Forgetting is a graded, evidence-bearing capability.** Hide $\neq$ abstract $\neq$ forget $\neq$ erase, with no implication among them, and each requiring an explicit witness and retained residual (0061, 0066, 0067, 0077, 0078).
- **Occurrence versus source identity.** Copy siblings force this distinction: equality of sources never licenses contraction, and a quotient that closes a cycle may be strictly coarser than the occurrence-level truth (0061, 0069, 0075, 0076, 0077, 0078).
- **Return versus period.** Checked finite histories admit boundary-return words but no intrinsic periodic dynamics; the missing object is a typed feedback/iteration operator, not another arithmetic formula (0073, 0074, 0072).
- **Feature extraction as spectral organisation of multivaluedness.** A resolution fibre can be replaced by a finite spectral invariant (Laurent polynomial, gcd/lcm/winding, monodromy characteristic) with an explicit residual, but never by unique reconstruction (0071, 0072, 0077, 0078).
- **One feature, three readings.** Construction/spatial/temporal readings of the same numerals or polynomial recur as the batch's candidate triadic structure, always conditional on a certificate identifying the readings (0071, 0072, 0078).
