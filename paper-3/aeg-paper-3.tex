\begin{abstract}
In a regular arithmetic expression space the eikonal identity
$|\nabla a|_g^2=\mu^2+\lambda^2a^2$ with $\mu\ne0$ forces the zero set to be a
curve; this paper develops the singular, automorphic, and parameterized zero
geometry that begins where that regularity fails, keeping real zero tubes,
complex root threads, and braid closures separate.
Holomorphic pullback is rigid: local degree $m$ produces a $2m$-pronged zero with
cone angle $2\pi m$, and for the normalized Hauptmodul of the $(2,4,\infty)$
Hecke group it gives a global singular space whose zero dessin, after bivalent
suppression, is the four-valent $\{\infty,4\}$ network.  The sign character of the Hecke group selects a
cover whose unit tangent bundle, under cusp compactification, is the torus-link
complement $S^3\setminus T(2,4)$; primitive
hyperbolic classes give periodic geodesic knots with the zero dessin as coding
spine, a synthesis of classical geodesic-flow and linking theorems rather than a
new invariant.  For threaded carriers $u^2=t^m$ the real carrier (genus $m-1$, four boundary
circles, $2m$ saddle reconnections) is separated from its intrinsic complex root
thread, which closes to $T(2,m)$; at $m=4$
factorization forgets a nonzero pure-braid linking class, and a four-strand
calibration identifies the unit-tangent central extension with a pullback of the
braid-center extension, whose integral relation residue transgresses to the
unit-tangent Euler class.  In the sparse sextic pencil $x^6-x-t$ with event
polynomial $Q_0(t)=t^5+5^5/6^6$, five star half-twists generate $B_6$, hence
every genus-two mapping class and the full $\operatorname{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)$
Gauss--Manin image; within the degree-$1296$ Lyashko--Looijenga fiber an explicit
second realization of $Q_0$ has, at $t=1$, a different Igusa--Clebsch ratio, so
the event schedule provably forgets complex moduli, and a monodromy-invariant
finite-fiber variance with an exact positive lower bound at $Q_0$ quantifies the
loss.  Compact, boundaryless, oriented zero tubes
over a circle are unions of tori, and affine conjugation recovers an Alexander quandle
whose ordinary torsion is exact while its resonant twisted class collapses to the
coloring count.  Unrestricted history naturality, an energetic (not logarithmic) reading of the
variance, and separation beyond Alexander or Burau remain open.
\end{abstract}
