\section{Origin of the problem: distilling the Quantale}

Christopher Mulvey introduced the Quantale (quantum lattice) to seek a
pointless-topology (locale) spectral duality for non-commutative $C^*$-algebras.
But because non-commutative multiplication destroys the idempotence of the meet
($a \wedge a = a$), the geometric intuition that ``algebraic elements correspond
to regions of space'' is obstructed, and the programme stalls on spectral
reconstruction.  Conversely, the Quantale finds a redundancy-free home in
generalised metric geometry (Lawvere metric spaces) and in resource/program logic
(Lambek calculus, Hoare weakest preconditions).  Its true core is \emph{not}
``reconstructing the points of a space'', but rather: \textbf{the distributivity
of non-commutative process composition over the extrema ($\bigvee/\inf$) is
equivalent to the existence of two-sided resource residuals ($a \backslash c,\
c / b$)}.

Introducing the standard Quantale into AEG to characterise resource complexity
and the computational spacetime duality produces three redundancies and
conflicts:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Over-axiomatised complete lattice}: demanding arbitrary joins
($\bigvee$) at a discrete finite syntactic layer forces a single arithmetic
expression to degenerate into a formal power set, masking the concrete geometric
tree/graph structure.
\item \textbf{Conflation of scalar cost and non-commutative curvature}: scalar
cost accumulation is commutative, whereas AEG's core non-commutativity stems from
the Lie-bracket defect ($[A, M] \neq 0$) of the affine generators (addition and
multiplication); a single semigroup multiplication cannot carry both at once.
\item \textbf{A single multiplication cannot separate the spacetime dimensions}:
one-dimensional semigroup composition only encodes serial temporal composition,
and cannot endogenously represent the cutting-interface width (Space / Cut
Capacity) of multi-branch suspension.
\end{enumerate}

One must therefore strip the pointless-topology shell of the traditional
Quantale, and, combining it with AEG's vision of a \emph{native nonlinear
language}, rebuild a minimal algebraic-geometric duality kernel.

\section{The core dialectic and the five-step derivational thread}

The convergence of this axiom system passed through a rigorous examination and
adjudication of five key theoretical forks:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Logical anchoring of spacetime duality (history-context adjunction
first).}  Take the two-ended evaluation pairing (Chu construction / Galois
polarity) of prefix evaluation history $\cH$ (Time) and suffix context / cutting
interface $\cK$ (Space) as the first-principles base.  No complete lattice is
presupposed; the residual-closure fixed points auto-generate the complete-lattice
structure.  The cross-section geometry of a continuous contact manifold then
emerges endogenously from this discrete adjoint structure under a state metric.
\item \textbf{Native nonlinear syntax and the crisis of the linear distributivity
law.}  Abandon the traditional ``pure-tree syntax plus patches'' route; natively
allow multiple fan-out of directed acyclic graphs ($\Delta: u \mapsto (u, u)$) in
the definition of the cutting interface (Cut $[m]$).  The derivation shows: once a
context internally contains nonlinear reuse (e.g.\
$k(\square) = \square \times (\square + 1)$), the evaluation pairing produces a
diagonal coupling that directly breaks the syntactic-level linear distributivity
$\langle h_1 \vee h_2, k \rangle = \langle h_1, k \rangle \vee \langle h_2, k
\rangle$.  The way out is to lift the pairing to a \emph{tropical wavefront
function module} over the cross-section state space, recovering strict
distributivity by operator-presheaf linearisation.
\item \textbf{First-class fan-out singularities and the condensation phase
transition.}  Refuse to treat variable reuse as a zero-cost syntactic wire;
establish the fan-out $\Delta: [1] \to [2]$ and the erasure $\epsilon: [1] \to
[0]$ as basic generators carrying positive action cost.  This yields the
\emph{condensation phase-transition criterion (the holographic scissors gap)} of
the nonlinear language: only when the history action $\cA(f)$ preparing some
state exceeds its instantaneous fan-out cost $c_\Delta(u)$ does the system
condense a high-curvature DAG sharing hub (corresponding to a two-dimensional
worldsheet handle, genus $g \ge 1$); shallow trivial computations
($\cA(f) < c_\Delta(u)$) spontaneously crack back into zero-curvature local pure
trees ($g = 0$).
\item \textbf{Semantic quotient calibration and the automatic emergence of
contact geometry.}  Fix the wire states to live in the \emph{minimal semantic
quotient space} $\PP^1(\QQ)$, and charge both the fan-out node $\Delta$ and the
arithmetic nodes $(+, \times)$ in proportion to the input state's information
height $I(u)$ (logarithmic Weil height).  This single decision solves three
problems at once:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Closing the physical loopholes}: it terminates both the BSS
unit-cost ``free lunch'' of repeated squaring fabricating exponential bit-width,
and the ``free $\gcd$ cancellation loophole'' on the quotient space, by charging
at the arithmetic-gate entrance in proportion to the full tube diameter.
\item \emph{Forcing contact geometry}: because the downstream fan-out cost
depends on the input state, the suffix context $k$ loses its fixed scalar cost
and becomes a state-dependent residual potential functional $\cW_k(u)$.  The
total-action extremal variation $\delta(z + \cW_k(u)) = 0$ directly induces the
contact $1$-form $dz + p\,dI = 0$ on the cross-section, where the conjugate
momentum $p = \partial \cW_k / \partial I$ exactly measures the fan-out load
amplification of the downstream context on the current wire's bit-width.
\item \emph{Revealing the associativity metric defect}: the value space $\QQ$
obeys strict associativity, but the context space $\cK_m$ --- because different
bracketing orders inflate the intermediate tube diameter or collapse by early
cancellation --- produces a nonzero action torsion (Associator Defect), forming a
non-strict Lax $2$-category.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Hyperbolic holographic calibration and the
scattering-wormhole picture (single hyperbolic base $\beta_1$).}  Facing the
fractal obstacle that the Weil height $I(p/q)$ is everywhere discontinuous on the
Euclidean real axis, refuse both the introduction of an external error parameter
for artificial smoothing and the inflation to an infinite-dimensional Adelic
product space.  Instead anchor the semantic boundary directly in a \emph{single
real hyperbolic upper half-plane $\HH^2$} (and its unit cotangent contact
manifold $S^*\HH^2$).  The information $I(u)$ is natively geometrised as the
smooth radial geodesic distance from the hyperbolic centre to the cusp Ford limit
circle; unary M\"obius transformations correspond to isometric flows of the bulk
space, while binary addition/multiplication and common-factor cancellation are
honestly represented as discrete scattering vertices on the hyperbolic worldsheet
and as cross-scale ``arithmetic wormholes''.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The formal axiom system: the Hyperbolic Tropical Wavefront--Residual
Core (HTWR Core)}

Let the scalar cost structure be the minimal Lawvere tropical semiring
$\cQ = ([0, \infty], \ge, +, 0)$ (order direction: $\inf$ as join, $+$ as
tensor).  The resource and spacetime duality of AEG is characterised by the
following four axioms.

\begin{axiom}[Semantic quotient state and hyperbolic holographic calibration]
\label{ax:state}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Markov semantic state}: the single-channel state space is the
projective rational quotient set $X := \PP^1(\QQ) = \QQ \cup \{\infty\}$.  Any
reduced element $u = p/q \in X$ ($\gcd(p,q)=1$) completely determines its
subsequent evolution cost, with no redundancy from path history.
\item \textbf{Hyperbolic--contact geometric anchoring}: endow the upper
half-plane $\HH^2$ with the standard hyperbolic metric $ds^2 = (dx^2 + dy^2)/y^2$
and the base point $z_0 = i$.  Each boundary cusp $u \in X$ is uniquely associated
with its tangent Ford limit circle $C_u \subset \HH^2$ (lifted to the standard
Legendrian curve in the unit cotangent sphere bundle
$S^*\HH^2 \cong \mathrm{PSL}(2, \RR)$).
\item \textbf{Information tube diameter (radial depth)}: the intrinsic
information of a state $u$ is calibrated by the hyperbolic geodesic distance from
the base point to the limit circle $C_u$:
\[
I(u) := \frac{1}{2} d_{\HH^2}(i, C_u) + c_0 \asymp \ln \max(|p|, |q|)
\]
where the constant $c_0$ is normalised so that the trivial states satisfy
$I(0), I(\pm 1), I(\infty) \ge 0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Rigged worldsheet syntax with metric, and the local area action]
\label{ax:syntax}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Graded cross-section spaces and flux}: the transverse interface of
a computation is a graded object $[m]$ carrying $m$ live wires (state space
$X^m$).  The instantaneous spatial complexity (total transverse flux) of a
cross-section state tuple $\mathbf{u} = (u_1, \dots, u_m) \in X^m$ is the sum of
fat-tube circumferences:
\[
\Sigma(\mathbf{u}) := \sum_{j=1}^m I(u_j).
\]
\item \textbf{First-class topological generators}: the nonlinear DAG syntax
category is generated by four kinds of directed pair-of-pants (cobordisms) under
serial welding ($\rhd$) and parallel juxtaposition ($\otimes$):
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{geodesic propagator} $\gamma \in \mathrm{PSL}(2, \ZZ)$: $[1] \to
[1]$, evolving along an isometric flow of $\HH^2$;
\item \emph{binary scattering pants} $\star \in \{+, \times, \div\}$: $[2] \to
[1]$, performing arithmetic composition and the potential common-factor
cancellation transition;
\item \emph{nonlinear fan-out pants} $\Delta$: $[1] \to [2]$, $u \mapsto (u,u)$,
producing a worldsheet handle (genus $g \ge 1$) and cross-channel coupling;
\item \emph{thermodynamic erasure cap} $\epsilon$: $[1] \to [0]$, releasing a
live wire at the end of its life cycle.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Local worldsheet area action}: any basic generator $g: [r] \to [s]$
at input state $\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{in}} \in X^r$ consumes a strictly positive
local action:
\[
c_g(\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{in}}) := \epsilon_0 + \alpha_g \sum_{j=1}^r I(u_j)
\qquad (\epsilon_0 > 0,\ \alpha_g > 0).
\]
The total action $\cA$ of a general nonlinear network is the sum of the local
actions of all its internal generators (a shared upstream node is counted once).
\end{enumerate}
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Tropical wavefront module and entangling linearisation]
\label{ax:wavefront}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Prefix wavefront collapse}: the set
$\cH_m = \mathrm{Hom}([0], [m])$ of all prefix histories of output width $m$ is
collapsed, by the least-action principle, into the \emph{preparation wavefront
functional (Eikonal Wavefront)} in the free $\cQ$-module $\cQ^{X^m}$:
\[
V_m(\mathbf{u}) := \inf \big\{ \cA(h) \mid h \in \cH_m,\ \mathrm{val}(h) =
\mathbf{u} \big\}.
\]
A pure-tree juxtaposition with no shared ancestor corresponds to a \emph{tropical
separable product state}: $V(\mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_2) = V_1(\mathbf{u}_1) +
V_2(\mathbf{u}_2)$; a nonlinear DAG containing a $\Delta$ fan-out corresponds to a
\emph{tropical entangled state} concentrated on a low-dimensional algebraic
subvariety (e.g.\ $(\cT_\Delta V)(u_1, u_2) = V(u_1) + c_\Delta(u_1)$ when
$u_1 = u_2$, and $+\infty$ otherwise).
\item \textbf{Context Bellman transfer operator}: any nonlinear suffix context
$k \in \cK_m = \mathrm{Hom}([m], [1])$ induces a semantic map $f_k: X^m \to X$
and a state-dependent residual potential $\cW_k: X^m \to [0, \infty)$, lifted to a
module homomorphism $\cT_k : \cQ^{X^m} \to \cQ^X$:
\[
(\cT_k V)(y) := \inf_{\substack{\mathbf{u} \in X^m \\ f_k(\mathbf{u}) = y}}
\big\{ V(\mathbf{u}) + \cW_k(\mathbf{u}) \big\}.
\]
$\cT_k$ preserves strict distributivity over pointwise infima:
$\cT_k(\inf_\alpha V_\alpha) = \inf_\alpha \cT_k(V_\alpha)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Bellman--Galois two-sided residual and spacetime conservation]
\label{ax:residual}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Residual adjoint envelope}: by the order-preservation of $\cT_k$,
given a target state $y^* \in X$ and a total-action budget $B \in [0, \infty)$,
one uniquely induces the reverse residual operator (maximal admissible
prefix-cost envelope):
\[
\cR_{k, y^*}(B)(\mathbf{u}) :=
\begin{cases}
B - \cW_k(\mathbf{u}), & \text{if } f_k(\mathbf{u}) = y^*, \\
-\infty \ (\text{unreachable}), & \text{if } f_k(\mathbf{u}) \neq y^*.
\end{cases}
\]
The prefix wavefront and the suffix residual satisfy the duality solvability
criterion: the target $y^*$ is reachable within budget $B$ iff there exists a
cross-section state $\mathbf{u} \in X^m$ with
$V_m(\mathbf{u}) \le \cR_{k, y^*}(B)(\mathbf{u})$.  By the Diophantine-geometric
\emph{Northcott theorem}, the effective residual support set
$\{\mathbf{u} \mid \cR_{k, y^*}(B)(\mathbf{u}) \ge 0\}$ is strictly finite for
any finite budget.
\item \textbf{Endogenous spacetime tradeoff law}: when the spatial cross-section
flux constraint $\Sigma(\mathbf{u}) \le \Sigma_{\max}$ is imposed, any deep hub
state $u$ that satisfies the condensation condition $\cA(f) > c_\Delta(u)$ but
exceeds the residual flux quota cannot survive crossing the cross-section; it is
forced to be erased by $\epsilon$ and recomputed after unfolding in the
downstream context --- thereby quantitatively converting the compression of the
transverse spatial flux into a rigid growth of the longitudinal total action
$\cA$ (time).
\end{enumerate}
\end{axiom}
