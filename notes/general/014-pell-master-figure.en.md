## 2. Panel (e): the four-gate crossing and the two `ln Q_k` cancellations

Let $x_n = P_n/Q_n$, resident catalyst $x_k = P_k/Q_k$, and set $c_k := 2 - x_k^2$. The Pell norm identity

$$P_k^2 - 2Q_k^2 = (-1)^k$$

gives the catalyst depth

$$c_k = 2 - \frac{P_k^2}{Q_k^2} = \frac{2Q_k^2 - P_k^2}{Q_k^2} = \frac{(-1)^{k+1}}{Q_k^2}.$$

### 2.1 Four-gate algebra (exact)

1. **additive dive** $z_1 = x_n + x_k = \dfrac{P_n Q_k + P_k Q_n}{Q_n Q_k} = \dfrac{Q_{n+k}}{Q_n Q_k}$ (using $Q_{n+k} = P_n Q_k + P_k Q_n$);
2. **reciprocal** $z_2 = \dfrac{1}{z_1} = \dfrac{Q_n Q_k}{Q_{n+k}}$;
3. **multiply by catalyst** $z_3 = z_2 c_k = \dfrac{Q_n Q_k}{Q_{n+k}}\cdot\dfrac{(-1)^{k+1}}{Q_k^2} = \dfrac{(-1)^{k+1} Q_n}{Q_k\, Q_{n+k}}$;
4. **add catalyst to close** $z_4 = z_3 + x_k = \dfrac{(-1)^{k+1} Q_n}{Q_k Q_{n+k}} + \dfrac{P_k}{Q_k} = \dfrac{(-1)^{k+1} Q_n + P_k Q_{n+k}}{Q_k Q_{n+k}}$.

The point: the heavy main variable $x_n$ occurs **only once** in the linear-fraction form $x_{n+k} = x_k + (2 - x_k^2)/(x_n + x_k)$ (zero fan-out on the main wire), so four gates cross $k$ steps.

### 2.2 Wormhole #1 (gate 3, cancels one `Q_k`)

Before cancellation, the numerator of $z_2$ carries one $Q_k$ and the denominator of $c_k$ carries $Q_k^2$, so the product is

$$z_3^{\text{(uncancelled)}} = \frac{(-1)^{k+1} Q_n Q_k}{Q_{n+k} Q_k^2}.$$

Cancelling the one common $Q_k$ gives $z_3 = (-1)^{k+1} Q_n/(Q_k Q_{n+k})$. The information height $I(p/q) = \ln\max(|p|,|q|)$ is denominator-dominated, so

$$I\big(z_3^{\text{(uncancelled)}}\big) = \ln\big(Q_{n+k} Q_k^2\big), \qquad I(z_3) = \ln\big(Q_{n+k} Q_k\big),$$

**depth saved** $= \ln\big(Q_{n+k}Q_k^2\big) - \ln\big(Q_{n+k}Q_k\big) = \ln Q_k.$

### 2.3 Wormhole #2 (gate 4, Cassini kills the other `Q_k`)

The Pell Cassini identity

$$P_{n+k} Q_k - P_k Q_{n+k} = (-1)^{k+1} Q_n$$

rewrites the numerator of $z_4$ as

$$(-1)^{k+1} Q_n + P_k Q_{n+k} = P_{n+k} Q_k,$$

so

$$z_4 = \frac{P_{n+k} Q_k}{Q_k Q_{n+k}} = \frac{P_{n+k}}{Q_{n+k}} = x_{n+k},$$

the second $Q_k$ cancels. The height is numerator-dominated ($P_{n+k} > Q_{n+k}$):

$$I\big(z_4^{\text{(uncancelled)}}\big) = \ln\big(P_{n+k} Q_k\big), \qquad I(x_{n+k}) = \ln P_{n+k},$$

**depth saved** $= \ln Q_k.$

### 2.4 Conclusion and numerics

The two cancellations each save exactly $\ln Q_k$, **in total $2\ln Q_k$** — the gate-by-gate arithmetic of the "cancellation wormhole collapses the bloated flux" line in Note II's table. Numerics (script self-check):

| quantity | $k=2$ ($x_3\to x_5$) | $k=8$ ($x_4\to x_{12}$) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| depth path | $1.946 \to 3.367 \to 3.367 \to 4.060 \to 3.714$ | $2.833 \to 5.953 \to 5.953 \to 13.063 \to 9.883$ |
| saved each | $\ln Q_2 = \ln 2 \approx 0.693$ | $\ln Q_8 = \ln 408 \approx 6.011$ |
| saved total | $2\ln 2 \approx 1.386$ | $2\ln 408 \approx 12.023$ |
| gates vs CF | $4$ vs $6$ ($1.5\times$) | $4$ vs $24$ ($6\times$) |

> **Honesty note**: the "saves $\ln Q_k$" of §2.2/§2.3 is exact at the step "uncancelled → one $Q_k$ cancelled"; further reduction to lowest terms may carry an extra incidental $O(1)$ factor such as $\gcd(Q_n,\, Q_k Q_{n+k})$ (not changing the leading $\ln Q_k$ term). For $k=8,n=4$, the state $z_3$: uncancelled height $\ln(Q_{n+k}Q_k^2)=\ln(13860\cdot408^2)\approx21.558$; after cancelling one $Q_k$: $\ln(Q_{n+k}Q_k)=\ln(13860\cdot408)\approx15.547$ (saves $6.011=\ln408$); then cancelling the incidental $\gcd=12$ gives the reduced $-1/471240$ at depth $13.063$. The state $z_4$ has no incidental factor (since $\gcd(P,Q)=1$) and saves exactly $\ln Q_k$. The "ghost" markers in the figure indicate "reduced depth $+\ln Q_k$".

## 3. Panel (f): the Sylvester no-wormhole control

Sylvester's $s_{n+1} = s_n^2 - s_n + 1$ has pairwise-coprime terms, **no norm identity**, hence no lightweight catalyst $c_k = \pm 1/Q_k^2$ and no cancellation wormhole to exploit. Its depth $\ln s_n$ **doubles every step** ($0.69 \to 1.10 \to 1.95 \to 3.76 \to 7.50 \to 15.00 \to 30.00$), in contrast with Pell's linear depth. Conclusion (per Note IV's positioning): in Sylvester the "repeated-squaring fabricates exponential bit-width" free lunch is blocked by per-tube-diameter charging — **naive = optimal**.
