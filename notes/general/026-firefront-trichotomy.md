## 1. The dictionary

The fewnomial rule of [025](025-quadratic-fewnomial-minus.md) separates three roles of the coefficients of $f(V)=aV^2+bV+c$ on $|V|=1$. On the hyperbolic fire-front $M=\mathbb H^2/\Gamma$ (torsion-free, basepoint $p$), the three roles have exact counterparts:

| role | fewnomial (025) | fire-front (hyperbolic) |
|---|---|---|
| **birth** (history/scale) | $\|b/a\|=2$ | $t=\|\gamma\|/2$ (crossing born) |
| **node / value** | node at $c-a$ (a **fixed point**) | crossing on the **fixed geodesic** $B_\gamma=\{d(p,z)=d(\gamma^{-1}p,z)\}$ |
| **root / zero** | roots product $c/a$ | wavefront through $\gamma p$ at $t=\|\gamma\|$ |

where $\|\gamma\|=d(p,\gamma p)$ is the displacement (the based length-spectrum entry).

**Two structural shifts** are worth stating precisely (`PROVED` / `STANDARD CONSEQUENCE`):

1. **fixed point → fixed geodesic.** In the fewnomial the parameter is the coefficient $b$; the node is a *point* $c-a$ that does not move with $b$. In the fire-front the parameter is the radius $t$; varying $t$ sweeps the crossing *along* the perpendicular bisector $B_\gamma$, so the value-level invariant is the whole geodesic $B_\gamma$ (equivalently its boundary endpoints $(\xi_1,\xi_2)$, the "directions" of [023](023-gauss-word-alignment.md)).

2. **half vs full.** The crossing is born at **half** the displacement ($t=\|\gamma\|/2$, the two spheres $\Sigma_t(p),\Sigma_t(\gamma^{-1}p)$ become tangent at the midpoint), and the wavefront passes through the deck image $\gamma p$ at the **full** displacement ($t=\|\gamma\|$). The two special times are paired:
   $$\text{birth} = \tfrac12\|\gamma\|,\qquad \text{root} = \|\gamma\|.$$
   Equivalently the self-intersection count is the *root count at double time*:
   $$N(t)=\#\{\gamma:\|\gamma\|<2t\}=\#\{\gamma p\ \text{reached by time}\ 2t\}.$$

## 2. The concrete example $\gamma=A$ (the parabolic $z\mapsto z+2$)

Take $p=i$; $A p=2+i$, $A^{-1}p=-2+i$, $\|A\|=\operatorname{arcosh}3=2\operatorname{arsinh}1\approx1.7627$. The bisector $B_A$ is the vertical geodesic $x=-1$ (endpoints $(-1,\infty)$, cf. [023](023-gauss-word-alignment.md)).

- **birth** at $t=\|A\|/2=\operatorname{arsinh}1\approx0.8814$: the two hyperbolic circles $\Sigma_t(p)$ (centre $i\sqrt2$, radius $1$) and $\Sigma_t(A^{-1}p)$ (centre $-2+i\sqrt2$, radius $1$) are tangent at the midpoint $-1+i\sqrt2\in B_A$.
- **root** at $t=\|A\|=\operatorname{arcosh}3\approx1.7627$: $\Sigma_t(p)$ (centre $3i$, radius $2\sqrt2$) passes through $A p=2+i$.
