\begin{abstract}
An arithmetic expression is a word of operations; a motion is a flow on a
manifold.  For expressions whose evaluation has a single internal spine, the two
are one structure: affine for addition and multiplication, projective once the
second operand slot is divided, contact-geometric in the order defect.  A
regular arithmetic expression space (AES) is an oriented Riemannian surface
$(M,g)$ with a smooth assignment $a\colon M\to\mathbb R$ satisfying the eikonal
equation $|\nabla a|_g^2=\mu^2+\lambda^2a^2$; the four operations then act as
motions translating and scaling $a$.  In the basic model $\mathfrak E_0$, the
upper half-plane with the left-invariant metric of
$\mathrm{Aff}^+(1,\mathbb R)$ (curvature $-\lambda^2$), addition and positive
multiplication are affine translations and dilations obeying a
Baumslag--Solitar law, and $a$ is a Laplace--Beltrami eigenfunction of
eigenvalue $2\lambda^2$.  Left-expanded words stay in the affine Borel cell
fixing $\infty$; the right-expanded language, once second-slot division is
admitted, generates all of $\mathrm{PGL}_2(\mathbb R)$ through the rank-one
Bruhat decomposition, and pulling standard horocycles back along one operator
word gives the dual reading, ripple geometry.  Commutativizing the motions
by total charge yields the accumulative commutative space, where the order
defect of two histories is an exact weighted area, the tearing.  The same defect
is the constant $\mu\lambda$ in the bracket
$[D_u,D_v]=\mu\lambda\partial_a$ of the horizontal fields of
$\alpha=da-\mu\,du-\lambda a\,dv$, a contact form whenever $\mu\lambda\ne0$, and
it is the curvature of that horizontal connection; for $\mathfrak E_0$ the
basic model is proved to be the Reeb quotient of the contact model, and
fixed-charge tearing and fixed-state Reeb drift are Legendre dual readings of
the same curvature.  The generators $\partial_a$, $a\partial_a$, and
$a\log a\,\partial_a$ span an infinite-dimensional Lie algebra, so no
finite-dimensional Lie group carries all three ranks, and no observable of the
charge endpoint detects tearing.  For every finite puncture count a conformally
compactifiable punctured AES is constructed, with an explicit four-circle zero
locus and exact reconstruction from three-ended pants blocks.  All results are
elementary, planar, or local to a three-dimensional contact model.
\end{abstract}
