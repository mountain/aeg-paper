## Mathematical objects

### Bootstrap Zero kernel $\mathcal G_0$
- source: 0095
- definition: $\mathcal G_0=(\mathcal O_0,\mathcal T_0;\mathcal I_t,\mathcal I_K,\mathcal I_X,\mathcal I_{\mathrm{thr}},\mathcal I_{\mathrm{AM}})$ — one object language, one type language, and five declared interpreters. The two public forms are the shell word ${}_K[]_X()_t$ and the through cell $\langle L\mid R\rangle$; composition is exact incidence gluing, not an added textual operator. Nothing in $\mathcal G_0$ evaluates, denotes, proves, normalizes, or halts.

### Three-shell boundary $B_\partial$
- source: 0095
- definition: $B_\partial:={}_K[]_X()_t$ with atoms $b_K:=\{\}$, $b_X:=[\,]$, $b_t:=(\,)$; the only public shell word. Mixed or same-role nestings such as `{[]}`, `[()]`, `{{}}` are not raw forms. $\mathsf{Circle}$ means finite presentation and syntactic closure only, $\mathsf{Line}$ means exact occurrence identity only; neither asserts termination, repetition, or finite unfolding.

### Namespaces, domain roles, and $\operatorname{opp}$
- source: 0095
- definition: Pairwise disjoint countable name classes $\mathsf{TypeName},\mathsf{FunctionName},\mathsf{SourceName},\mathsf{OccurrenceName},\mathsf{IncidenceName},\mathsf{HoleName},\mathsf{CutName},\mathsf{CellName},\mathsf{StrandName},\mathsf{PolicyName},\mathsf{ThreadAtomName},\mathsf{InterpreterName},\dots$; reserved domain roles $d::=K\mid X\mid t$; for distinct roles $\operatorname{opp}(K,X)=t$, $\operatorname{opp}(X,t)=K$, $\operatorname{opp}(t,K)=X$, with $\operatorname{opp}(d,d)$ undefined. Display strings may coincide across classes; typed identities do not, and alpha-renaming never identifies sources, occurrences, incidences, holes, cuts, cells, or strands.

### Annotated incidence, frontier, hole context
- source: 0095
- definition: An annotated incidence is $i:(A,s,o)@_Qd^p$ recording value type $A$, source $s$, occurrence $o$, policy role $d$ and polarity $p\in\{+,-\}$; its underlying value type remains $A$, so $A@_Qd^p$ is an indexed form and not a member of `ValueType`. An incidence frontier is a finite ordered word $F::=\epsilon\mid\langle i:(A,s,o)@_Qd^p\rangle F$; a syntax-hole context is $H::=\epsilon_H\mid\langle h:A\rangle H$.

### Whole-cut (through) cell and ordered through type
- source: 0095, 0101, 0102
- definition: A raw cell is $c:\langle L\mid_n R\rangle[\tau]$ with finite ordered frontiers $L,R$, one named cut or middle-interface occurrence $n$, and a finite thread word $\tau$; compactly printed $\langle L\mid R\rangle$, but raw-syntax identity retains $c,n,L,R,\tau$ and equal printouts are not equal forms. The type form is $\mathsf{Through}_{de}^{f}(F_L,N_f,F_R)$ with $d\ne e$, $f=\operatorname{opp}(d,e)$; $de$ is ordered and $\mathsf{Through}_{de}^{f}\ne\mathsf{Through}_{ed}^{f}$. From 0101 the cell is atomic: one cut co-generates a continuation germ and a boundary germ, so "<L|R>" is not an operation on two pre-existing sides, and no single cell contains the triadic carrier.

### Thread word and typed thread atoms
- source: 0095, 0097

A word retains order and every repetition and is not an execution trace; no equation identifies duality, conjugation, polarity reversal, side reversal, or cut reversal.

### Signed crossing atom and crossing judgement
- source: 0095, 0097
- definition: For a frontier $F$ with complete incidence records $\rho=(i,s,o,A,d,p)$ at positions $r,r+1$ and $1\le r<|F|$, let $s_rF$ exchange the two whole records. Then $\Sigma;Q\vdash_{\mathsf{cross}}\mathsf{crossing}[\xi;r;\eta]:F\rightsquigarrow s_rF$ for $\eta\in\{\mathsf{over},\mathsf{under}\}$, where `over` means the strand initially at $r$ passes over the one at $r+1$. Both signs have the same routed target but are different raw atoms; a crossing never detaches, copies, discards, or recolours an incidence field.

### Raw braid block, endpoint purity, formal inverse
- source: 0095, 0097
- definition: $\beta::=\epsilon_\beta\mid\mathsf{crossing}[\xi;r;\eta]::\beta$ with formation $\Sigma;Q\vdash_{\mathsf{braid}}\beta:F\rightsquigarrow F'$ by composing adjacent crossing judgements in written order. A block with $F'=F$ is endpoint-pure, which is neither empty nor identity. $\operatorname{inv}_{\mathsf{syn}}(a_1::\cdots::a_n)=\overline{a_n}::\cdots::\overline{a_1}$ exchanges `over`/`under` while keeping crossing name and gap; the empty word is fixed. No cancellation $\beta::\operatorname{inv}_{\mathsf{syn}}(\beta)=\epsilon_\beta$, no Artin relation, no unsigned-swap quotient.

### Finite incidence diagram; line and circle values
- source: 0095
- definition: A raw form is $P=\operatorname{diagram}(B_\partial;\mathcal C,\mathcal E;\partial^-P,\partial^+P)$ with finite name-indexed families of cells $\mathcal C$ and strands $\mathcal E$, an explicit finite incidence table, ordered exposed frontiers, and every name and unused incidence retained; adjacency is gluing a positive exposed incidence to a compatible negative one, juxtaposition is disjoint union, and no associativity or interchange law is asserted. ## Results

### Finite minimality of six
- source: 0101
- kind: theorem
- status: proved by finite counting under six explicitly stated contract clauses; failure modes named
- statement: If a triadic cyclic carrier ($m=3$ role interfaces, each once) has binary cuts creating two distinct cooriented named ports, every port positively occupied, no implicit contraction before allocation, and one occurrence ledger shared by the line and circle readings, then the number $n$ of degree occurrences satisfies $n\ge 2m$, so $n_{\min}=6$, attained by one occurrence per port.
- engineering dependency: none

### Alternating two-matching bridge to an open $M_6$ boundary
- source: 0104
- kind: theorem
- status: finite graph theorem under explicit formation hypotheses; executable calibration is exhaustive only for the presented six-element matching fixture
- statement: Let $\kappa,\tau$ be the fixed-point-free involutions induced by three whole-cut cells and three through channels on the six-port ledger, both total, with no cut pair equal to a through pair, and their union connected; then $G=(P,E_\kappa\cup E_\tau)$ is $C_6$ with alternating edge kinds, so the carrier presents $\mathsf{bridge}(\mathbb W_6)=M_6^\partial[\kappa\tau\kappa\Rightarrow\tau\kappa\tau]$. Cardinality six alone is insufficient.
- engineering dependency: partial

## Threads
- **Syntax before semantics, with the barrier written down.** Every mathematical note in the batch is deliberately weaker than a language: no evaluation, denotation, truth, transition relation, or halting judgement is introduced, and each note ends with an explicit list of what does *not* follow (0095 §0, 0096 §7, 0097 §6, 0098 §6, 0099 §9, 0101 §0, 0102 §14, 0104 §8). The recurring form of a contribution is a formation judgement plus a non-derivation list.
- **One authoritative occurrence-and-multiplicity ledger.** From 0098's source/occurrence split through 0101's $\mathbb W_6$ to 0102's production/degree ledgers and 0103–0104's views, the invariant is: no view, braid, collision, projection, or quotient may create, drop, rename, or merge an occurrence. Copies need production witnesses; collisions conserve multiplicity; views must literally reuse the ledger ($V_\nu(\partial c)=\partial V_\nu(c)$).
- **Algebraic separation discipline.** A fixed family of distinctions is defended note after note: role vs value type, $de$ vs $ed$, side vs incidence polarity vs crossing sign vs motion, source vs occurrence vs hole vs incidence vs port, `role[d]` vs `history[ζ]`, `add`/`mul` vs $\lor$/$\land$, merge vs forgetting vs dissipation vs retraction, empty vs balanced vs observed zero, returning endpoint vs empty history vs witnessed zero.
- **Rebase from factor inventory to a single carrier.** 0095–0100 place factors side by side (with 0100 declaring the inventory "closed"); 0101 re-reads the same factors as projections of one six-port whole-cut carrier, and 0102–0104 rebuild the grammar, cell machinery, and bridge on that carrier. The earlier notes are explicitly retained, not erased, but demoted to factor-placement records.
- **Cut as atomic generator; line and circle as views.** One atomic $\langle L\mid R\rangle$ co-generates a continuation germ and a boundary germ; `Line` and `Circle` cease to be neighbouring value constructors and become checked projections of the carrier with declared kernels. This is the batch's central structural move.
- **Conditional minimality and relative universality.** "Why six?" is answered by a counting theorem under six named hypotheses ($n\ge 2m$), with failure modes published, and universality is downgraded to relative initiality in a category of presented models. The pattern — state the contract, prove the bound, name the countermodels — recurs in 0104's two-matching criterion.
- **Signed history vs Coxeter shadow.** 0097 keeps $\sigma_i,\sigma_i^{-1}$ distinct and forbids Artin and cancellation equations; 0103 shows how a finite $M_6$ face arises only after an explicit forgetting of winding, and separates signed routing, retraction-as-cancellation, and braid reorganization as three different acts.
- **Calibration is not theorem.** The batch repeatedly marks exhaustive finite computations (128 supports, 203 restricted-growth partitions, 729 fibre maps, 24-vertex permutohedron, 1063 passing tests) as exact statements about a fixture, and distinguishes them from claims about arbitrary diagrams; several no-go results, however, are *derived* from those enumerations and are stated as such.
- **Explicit residuals as the honesty mechanism.** Unaccounted ports, forgotten fibres, unselected alternatives, open relation boundaries, unclassified interaction, and prefix frontiers are all required to remain named, so that incompleteness is a recorded datum rather than an absence.
- **Extension envelope instead of silent ontology drift.** 0101–0104 fix a set of named extension slots (arity, topology, collision-theory, character, interpreter, equation, observer, proof, learning, omega, arithmetic, physical) and a preservation-obligation schema, with the standing rule that an extension must be visible at the point where it enters.
