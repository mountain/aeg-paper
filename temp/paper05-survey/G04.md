### Legendre cusp data and vanishing directions
- source: 0043-prism, 0044, 0047
- definition: Oriented primitive vanishing directions $\delta_0=(1,0)$, $\delta_1=(0,1)$, $\delta_\infty=(-1,-1)=-a-b$ in $\mathbf Z^2$, with symplectic form $J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbf Z)=\mathrm{Sp}(2,\mathbf Z)$ acting on the Legendre family $y^2=x(x-1)(x-\lambda)$.

### Transvection and positive unipotent cusp lift
- source: 0043-prism, 0047, 0048
- definition: $L_\delta=I-\delta(J\delta)^{T}$ (primitive positive transvection) and $U_\delta=L_\delta^{2}$, giving $U_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, $U_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}$, $U_\infty=\begin{pmatrix}-1&2\\-2&3\end{pmatrix}$.

### The two closure conventions
- source: 0043-prism, 0044
- definition: Positive-unipotent lifts satisfy $U_0U_1U_\infty=-I$; compatible actual monodromies $M_0=U_0$, $M_1=U_1$, $M_\infty=-U_\infty$ satisfy $M_0M_1M_\infty=I$. They are projectively equal at each cusp and differ only in the infinity-chart central sign.

### The three typed carriers $K,X,t$
- source: 0043-prism
- definition: $K$: construction states $k=(q,p,h)$ with $q\in\mathbf Z^2$, a concrete presentation $p$, and an exact finite history $h$. $X$: oriented homology charges $x=q\in\mathbf Z^2$. $t$: period covectors $\ell\in(\mathbf Z^2)^*$. Crossing acts by $J_c^K(q,p,h)=(M_cq,p,h\cdot[c])$, $J_c^X(q)=M_cq$, $J_c^t(\ell)=M_c^{-T}\ell$.

- source: 0048-energy

## Results

### Through-crossing is a cospan, not a transport arrow
- source: 0047
- kind: proposition
- status: main structural thesis of the note; bounded, no general calculus claimed
- statement: For a singular value $c$, the elementary object relating incoming to outgoing data is the pinch cospan $F_-\to F_0\leftarrow F_+$ together with the surgery trace, not an unqualified arrow $F_-\to F_+$; a deterministic continuation exists only after choosing a smoothing/gluing phase.
- engineering dependency: partial

### Monodromy is the difference of two through-resolutions
- source: 0047
- kind: proposition
- status: key new relation; stated with a fixed orientation convention
- statement: With $P^\uparrow,P^\downarrow:F_-\to F_+$ the marked isomorphisms obtained by bypassing the node above and below, $(P^\downarrow)^{-1}P^\uparrow=T_\delta$; equivalently, two resolutions satisfy $(P_{\theta_2})^{-1}P_{\theta_1}=T_\delta^{\,\theta_1-\theta_2}$. So around-monodromy measures the ambiguity between two through-resolutions, not an unrelated extra effect.
- engineering dependency: none

### Local Legendre reduction and the squared cusp
- source: 0047
- kind: proposition
- status: identity verified symbolically by the executable test
- statement: The Legendre family near $\lambda=0,x=0$ is $UV=\lambda^2$; the parameter is a quadratic cover of the elementary semistable coordinate $s=UV$, so a loop of winding one in $\lambda$ is a double loop in $s$, whence $M_{\mathrm{Legendre}}=T_\delta^{2}=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ for $\delta=(1,0)^{T}$.
- engineering dependency: partial

### Finite vertex closure does not imply lifted path closure
- source: 0048-cellular
- kind: proposition
- status: established in the finite cubical model with explicit all-degree checks
- statement: The vertex action satisfies $D_0^n=I$ while the cellular one-chain action satisfies $D_1^n\ne I$, and on homology $M_D^n=\begin{pmatrix}1&n\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$; the unbounded winding is stored in integer path coefficients, requiring no infinite cellulation.
- engineering dependency: partial

### Picard–Lefschetz matrix derived from a cellular shear
- source: 0048-cellular
- kind: proposition
- status: exact cellular chain computation, verified to be invertible in all three degrees
- statement: The shear $D$ is an exact invertible relative cellular chain representative of the Dehn twist, satisfying $\partial D_2=D_1\partial$ and $\partial D_1=D_0\partial$; after boundary gluing $gD_A=D_Tg$ and the induced action on $H_1(T_n)$ is $D_*(a)=a$, $D_*(b)=b+a$, i.e. $D_*(x)=x+\langle\delta,x\rangle\delta$, symplectic with $M_D^{T}JM_D=J$.
- engineering dependency: essential

### Specialization forgets exactly the vanishing direction
- source: 0048-cellular
- kind: proposition
- status: exact on the finite nodal quotients
- statement: The pinch $q:T_n\to N_T$ kills the vanishing cycle, $q_*(a)=0$, so because $D_*(b)=b+a$ one has $q_*D_*=q_*$; with the transported marking $q_+=q_-D^{-1}$ the strict marked identity $q_+D=q_-$ holds, and two marked resolutions differ by $P_\ell^{-1}P_k=D^{k-\ell}$.
- engineering dependency: partial

### Zero directional energy characterizes exact invariance

### Space–time crossing square is symplectic duality
- source: 0043-prism
