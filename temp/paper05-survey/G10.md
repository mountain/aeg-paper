### Hole-polarity conjugacy and its uniqueness
- source: 0106b
- kind: proposition
- status: exact symbolic proof, plus exhaustive six-port audit
- statement: For $\rho_h=\kappa_h=(h_1^+h_1^-)(h_2^+h_2^-)(h_3^+h_3^-)$ one has $\rho_h\kappa_h\rho_h^{-1}=\kappa_h$, $\rho_h\tau_+\rho_h^{-1}=\tau_-$, $\rho_h^2=1$.
