## Mathematical objects

### Observation form
- source: 0049
- definition: A finite observer-indexed form $\mathfrak F_Q=(T_Q,X_Q,K_Q;\operatorname{Obs}_Q)$ with a typed observation judgment $\operatorname{Obs}_Q:T_Q\times X_Q\times K_Q\to\operatorname{ObservationResult}_Q$, where the result distinguishes $\operatorname{Positive}(e)$ (carrying evidence), $\operatorname{Refuted}(c)$ (requiring a finite exhaustive or negative certificate), and $\operatorname{Unknown}(b)$ (retaining the boundary that blocked a decision). The product notation is explicitly flagged as shorthand, not a claim about tensor products of a frontier.

### Opposite-pair sections
- source: 0049, 0050 (residual activation), 0057
- definition: Fixing two coordinates gives a raw section on the third: $S_t(x,k)=\operatorname{Obs}_Q(-,x,k)$, $S_X(t,k)=\operatorname{Obs}_Q(t,-,k)$, $S_K(t,x)=\operatorname{Obs}_Q(t,x,-)$. Key correction: $S_D\ne\text{computer }D$ — a section is an *environment*, not a program.

### Injected program triple and code carriers
- source: 0049
- definition: Local state carriers $S_t,S_X,S_K$, typed code carriers $\operatorname{Code}_t,\operatorname{Code}_X,\operatorname{Code}_K$, and $p=(p_t,p_X,p_K)$ with $p_D\in\operatorname{Code}_D$; code identity, program identity, value equality, and observational equivalence are kept distinct.

### Typed interpreter
- source: 0049
- definition: $U_D:(\operatorname{Code}_D,S_D,\operatorname{Env}_D,\operatorname{Fuel})\to\operatorname{RunResult}_D$, with $\operatorname{Env}_t=S_t(s_X,s_K)$ etc.; results are $\text{Halted},\text{Suspended},\text{Failed},\text{Unknown}$, and fuel exhaustion returns `Unknown` rather than a divergence proof.

### Scheduler
- source: 0049, 0050 (residual activation)
- definition: $D_n=\sigma(n,\operatorname{Trace}_{<n})\in\{t,X,K\}$ together with a snapshot discipline; a run is a partial transition sequence $(p,s_0,Q,\sigma)\rightsquigarrow s_1\rightsquigarrow s_2\rightsquigarrow\cdots$. A synchronous round is a *derived* macrostep (freeze, run three, collect, commit at a barrier), never primitive.

### Startup calibration
- source: 0049
- definition: $\operatorname{Cal}_0$ checking code versions, interpreter compatibility, port types, initial states, conventions, observer policy, snapshot discipline, fuel units, energy scales, and source/occurrence authority; it returns a report that may be `Unknown` or carry a residual mismatch.

### Universality ladder U0–U3
- source: 0049
- definition: U0 uniform programmability (three independently chosen code values, three interpreters, one scheduler, one report schema, honest `Unknown`); U1 component universality ($\forall P\in\mathcal P_D\ \exists\ulcorner P\urcorner\in\operatorname{Code}_D$ with $U_D(\ulcorner P\urcorner;s,e)\simeq P(s,e)$); U2 coupled universality ($M\simeq_Q\operatorname{Run}(U_t,U_X,U_K;p_t,p_X,p_K;\sigma)$); U3 reflection (code installed only by a certified transition $p_{n+1}=\operatorname{Install}(p_n,\operatorname{Proposal}_K,\operatorname{Certificate})$). Claimed ordering U0 $<$ U1 $<$ U2 with U3 as an orthogonal axis.

### LABS energy functional and its exact local calculus

- source: 0050 (labs)
- definition: For $s\in\{-1,+1\}^n$, $C_k(s)=\sum_{i=0}^{n-k-1}s_is_{i+k}$ and $E(s)=\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}C_k(s)^2$, with display-only merit factor $F(s)=n^2/(2E(s))$; the search minimises the integer $E$. A single flip at $j$ changes correlations by $\Delta_jC_k=-2s_j(\mathbf 1_{j\ge k}s_{j-k}+\mathbf 1_{j+k<n}s_{j+k})$, hence $\Delta_jE=\sum_k(2C_k\Delta_jC_k+(\Delta_jC_k)^2)$, an exact $O(n)$ computation. $E$ is invariant under global sign reversal, sequence reversal, and multiplication by $(-1)^i$; these generate an 8-element orbit deduplicated in a bounded archive by a canonical representative, while trajectory identity and orbit identity remain different data.

### Activation context

Under $p_t:t\mapsto1$, $p_X:(t,x,k)\mapsto x+tk$: $\sigma_{\min}=(t,X)$ separates, $(X,t)$ does not, and $(X,t,X)$ does.

### Triadic transformation monoid
- source: 0051, 0052, 0053, 0056
- definition: Generators $T(t,x,k)=(1,x,k)$, $X(t,x,k)=(t,t\wedge k,k)$, $K(t,x,k)=(t,x,1-k)$ on $\{0,1\}^3$; $M=\langle T,X,K\rangle\subseteq\operatorname{End}(S_0)$ with $|M|=16$, closed under composition, maximum shortest representative length 4. Satisfies $T^2=T$, $X^2=X$, $K^2=1$, $TK=KT$, while $TX\ne XT$ and $KX\ne XK$.

### Direct proposition language
- source: 0051
- definition: $\mathcal L_X^{\mathrm{ctx}}=\{P_m\mid m\in M\}$ has exactly 5 distinct elements: $x$, $t\wedge k$, $t\wedge\neg k$, $k$, $\neg k$ (shortest contexts $\varepsilon,X,KX,TX,TKX$ with step-count vectors). ### Contextual equivalence and Boolean envelope
- source: 0051, 0052
- definition: $s\sim_{\mathrm{ctx}}s'\iff\forall m\in M,\ x(m(s))=x(m(s'))$; the classes are exactly $\{(t,x,k,0),(t,x,k,1)\}$, eight of them. Then $\mathcal B_X^{\mathrm{ctx}}=\operatorname{Bool}(\mathcal L_X^{\mathrm{ctx}})\cong\mathcal P(\widetilde S/{\sim_{\mathrm{ctx}}})\cong\mathcal P(S_0)$ with $|\mathcal B_X^{\mathrm{ctx}}|=2^8=256$, strictly smaller than $|\mathcal P(\widetilde S)|=2^{16}=65\,536$.

### State transport and weakest precondition
- source: 0052
- definition: Semantic entailment $A\models B\iff A\subseteq B$; state transport $A\xrightarrow{F}_{\mathrm{state}}B\iff F(A)\subseteq B$ for $F\in M$; Hoare triple $\{A\}F\{B\}$ with exact equivalence $\{A\}F\{B\}\iff A\subseteq F^{-1}(B)=\operatorname{wp}_F(B)$.

### Transport reachability preorder
- source: 0052
- definition: $A\leadsto B\iff\exists F\in M,\ F(A)\subseteq B$; reflexive and transitive (identity in $M$, $M$ composition-closed) but **not antisymmetric**, since $k\leadsto\neg k$ and $\neg k\leadsto k$ via $K$. It classifies dynamical reachability, not logical equivalence.

### Evidence fibre and evidence transformer (proposed)
- source: 0052
- definition: Two-sorted carrier: for each proposition $A$, a typed evidence carrier with projection $\pi_A:E_A\to S_0$, support $|A|=\{s\mid E_A(s)\ne\varnothing\}$. Ordinary proof transformer: $\Phi:E_A\to E_B$ with $\pi_B\circ\Phi=\pi_A$; action-indexed transformer: $\Phi_F$ with $\pi_B\circ\Phi_F=F\circ\pi_A$. Equal supports need not give equal evidence carriers.

### Context presentation
- source: 0053, 0056
- definition: For $m\in M$ and $s\in P_m$, $E_m(s)=\{(w,\rho_w(s))\mid \llbracket w\rrbracket=m\}$ with $\rho_w$ the full run trace; $E_m=\coprod_{s\in P_m}E_m(s)$ and $\pi_m(w,\rho_w(s))=s$. The semantic index is finite (16) while the fibre is infinite (loops such as $KK$ give unboundedly long words).

### Causal suffix continuation
- source: 0053, 0054 (tensor)
- definition: For a finite suffix $d$ with $m;d=n$ and $P_m\subseteq P_n$, $\tau_d:E_m\to E_n$, $\tau_d(w,\rho_w(s))=(wd,\rho_{wd}(s))$, so $\pi_n\circ\tau_d=\pi_m$ and the old trace remains a literal prefix. Deletion, replacement, insertion-before-the-past, and arbitrary support-table witness selection are forbidden.

### Synchronized tensor and linear evidence map
- source: 0054 (tensor), 0055
- definition: $(E_m\otimes_s E_n)(s)=E_m(s)\times E_n(s)$ over one shared initial state, with $Q(E_m\otimes_s E_n)=P_m\cap P_n$, $Q(I)=S_0$; 273 types ($1+16+16^2$). ### Typed vacuum atoms and three-bracket boundary

## Threads
- **Observation is not computation.** Opposite-pair sections supply environments; programs supply computation (0049). The whole 0050–0054 line is an exploration of what the observer/program split buys: activation of residuals, context-generated propositions, and the collapse of state transport into Hoare logic rather than entailment.
- **Order and schedule as semantic data.** $t;X\ne X;t$ (0050 residual), the 16-element monoid with $TK=KT$ but $TX\ne XT$, $KX\ne XK$ (0051), conditional exchange (0051/0052), suffix-ordered causal evidence (0053), and port routing in the synchronized tensor (0054 tensor) are successive refinements of one claim: order is derived from programs, not postulated as a global $L\mid R$.
- **Two residuals.** A distinction can be recovered by a later program (typed state, activation) or be permanently invisible to programs and visible only to a refined observer (audit history, full twist, pure-braid class). This is the recurring "observer refinement vs program activation" boundary (0050 residual, 0052, 0053, 0056, 0057, 0058).
- **Finite completeness is relative.** A finite language can be complete for a declared quotient while incomplete for the fine carrier (0051: 256 vs 65,536), for the presented evidence (0053: 72 vs 60 vs 24), and for arity/resource shape (0054 tensor: 11 supports for 273 types).
- **From extensional supports to proof-relevant evidence.** The 0052 no-go forces a two-sorted carrier; 0053 supplies open finite-word fibres and causal suffix morphisms; 0054 adds a synchronized multiplicative fragment; 0055/0056 force the index from monoid element to checked presentation. The residual of this thread is an honest gap: no evidence carrier is yet Rust-authoritative.
- **Structural rules as empirical questions.** Exchange holds exactly where a commutation equation is checked; contraction and weakening have no admitted witness even under equal support (0054 tensor); copy and discard appear only as certified program events (0055).
- **Decision quotient vs presentation.** 16 transformations decide reachability; presentations retain how evidence was produced (0056). The same tension recurs as support-vs-presentation (0053) and as value-vs-label vs label-vs-Audin (0057/0058).
- **Disciplined universality.** 0049's U0–U3 ladder, the repeated `Unknown` discipline, and the exhaustive nonclaims sections keep "uniform programmability" from being relabelled as universality; 0057/0058 locate the missing primitive (a history-sensitive observer) rather than asserting a substrate.
- **Reversibility versus computation.** Groups have no nonidentity idempotents (0057); reversible dilation needs a changing or growing boundary (0058). Noninvertible, idempotent, information-losing gates are the real computational content, and no braid-group or permutation layer can host them.
- **Witness benchmarks as calibration, not evidence.** LABS (0050 labs) and sorting networks (0054 sorting) are honest bounded calibrations of the three-program protocol; both explicitly refuse causal attribution without ablation and both return `Unknown` at budget exhaustion.
