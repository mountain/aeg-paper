### Finite thread adequacy theorem
- source: 0082
- kind: theorem
- status: proved by structural induction on $\varphi$ under A0–A6; exhaustive finite fixtures confirm
- statement: For every formula of the finite many-sorted grammar, every finite structure satisfying A0–A6 (well-sortedness, atomic exactness, resolved pair completeness, tagged sums, transformer completeness, domain exhaustiveness, alpha/substitution naturality) and every well-sorted environment, $\mathcal M,\rho\models\varphi\iff\operatorname{Fill}_{\mathcal M,\rho}(\varphi)\ne\varnothing$. This is semantic adequacy, not $\Gamma\models\varphi\Rightarrow\Gamma\vdash\varphi$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Fibre-natural substitution theorem
- source: 0082
- kind: theorem
- status: proved by simultaneous induction on terms and formulas; capture-avoiding case checked in the fixture
- statement: $\mathsf L_{\varphi[t/x]}(\mathcal M,\rho)\cong\mathsf L_\varphi(\mathcal M,\rho[x\mapsto\llbracket t\rrbracket_{\mathcal M,\rho}])$, strengthening the Tarskian substitution lemma $\mathcal M,\rho\models\varphi[t/x]\iff\mathcal M,\rho[x\mapsto\llbracket t\rrbracket]\models\varphi$.
- engineering dependency: none

### Adequacy does not give natural-deduction completeness
- source: 0082
- kind: no-go
- status: explicit boundary; pointwise transformers are not a uniform derivation
- statement: First-order entailment quantifies over all structures, while the adequacy theorem yields only a family of pointwise transformers $\prod_{\gamma\in\Gamma}\mathsf L_\gamma\to\mathsf L_\varphi$; hence finite thread adequacy $\ne$ soundness and completeness of natural deduction.
- engineering dependency: none

### Cartesian vs substructural logic-selection criterion
- source: 0082
- kind: proposal
- status: proposed criterion supported by explicit counterconditions
- statement: Copy, weakening and compatible pairing license Cartesian connectives; exclusive sources and restricted transformers license substructural (linear, affine, relevant, ordered) connectives. If incompatibility of conjunction witness pairs or absence of admissible transformers is intrinsic rather than an implementation defect, classical logic must not be forced.
- engineering dependency: none

### Support inclusion for conjunction is strict without compatibility
- source: 0081, 0082

- status: proved; decisive counterexample retained in the fixture
- statement: $\operatorname{Supp}_Q(A\land B)\subseteq\operatorname{Supp}_Q(A)\cap\operatorname{Supp}_Q(B)$, with equality only under compatible-pair completeness: whenever both fibres are inhabited there is at least one compatible pair. Two individually inhabited fibres can have an empty compatibility-restricted conjunction when both lines consume one exclusive source, so support intersection over-approximates proof-relevant conjunction.
- engineering dependency: none
