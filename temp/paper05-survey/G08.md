## Threads
- **Observer-relative closure over a retained residual.** From the marked witness of 0088 through the feature-closure judgment of 0089 and the planned carriers of 0090/0092: closing the world, closing a feature of the world, approximating under a measure, approaching a limit, and reopening under a stronger observer are five distinct modalities that must never be silently strengthened or collapsed.
- **Prefix-free residual geometry and the fibre under a scalar.** Cantor-boundary cylinders, antichain frontiers, Kraft capacity, and the requirement of a coverage certificate before $\mu(R_t)=\kappa(A_t)$; equal mass with unequal geometry is the batch's canonical "same scalar, different fibre" example (0089, 0090).
- **Skeleton/decoration separation.** The recurring move of erasing audited nominal data to a small structural skeleton and lifting back by noncanonical fresh decoration: $\Xi_Q$ projections and their congruences in 0087, and the erase/lift pair (Thms 3.1, 4.1) behind skeleton derivability in 0094.
- **Linearity and order as the source of the metatheorems.** No exchange, weakening, or contraction; copy/discard are explicit terms (0087); linearity is what makes the strong diamond, the SC subcases, and the leftmost-head invariant true (0093, 0094).
- **Endpoint versus history versus 2-cell.** A four-level ladder of increasingly retaining structure (proof endpoint, composite boundary transport, history, history 2-cell) that forbids reading confluence as coherence (0093), echoed by the refusal to identify long forms with their eta-expansions and the admission of multiple focused proofs (0094).
- **The $\Omega$ boundary discipline.** $\Omega$ appears as recursive syntax $\Omega_\Pi$ with no halting judgement (0087), as the halting measure $\Omega_U$ and its dyadic-cell tails (0089), and is explicitly *not* produced by finite confluence or finite focused search (0093 §14, 0094 §13.3–13.4): no compactification from finite experiments.
- **Endogenous generation as the missing motion.** The learning/proof duality of 0089 is corrected in 0091 into failure → imagine → trial → ledger → characteristic → vocabulary → failure′, with breakthrough as endogenously generated scope crossing rather than cardinality of an ambient space, tested by the $\sigma_1\to\sigma_2$ ladder of 0092.
- **Three-domain roles $K,X,t$ with complement typing.** Observer policy, incidence names and strands (0087), construction/time/frontier readings and the $(K,X,t)$ cost vector (0089, 0091), and the deferred seven-subset triadic extension (0090 §17).
- **Authority separation between generated syntax and checked semantics.** Repeatedly: a generated word has no semantic authority until it passes independent checking, and no observer quotient or right-to-forget may be declared before composition laws exist (0088–0094).
