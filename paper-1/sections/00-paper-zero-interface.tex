Paper~0 is the geometric foundation of this series.  It constructs the space in
which the elementary arithmetic operations act as motions, the dual reading of
those motions, the commutativization against which their order defect is
measured, and the contact structure of that defect.  It fixes explicit left- and
right-expanded conventions, proves the distributive expansion that relates two
expansions sharing an operator, and treats reciprocals, poles, the projective
point at infinity, affine and reciprocal fixed points, and the common
$2\times2$ matrix representation.

The present paper begins one level earlier and stays there.  Its first problem
is intrinsic: which finite binary expression trees have a unique legal order of
internal evaluation, including mixed operand-slot patterns?  The answer is the
single-spine classification.  Choosing an accumulator then gives the marked
spinal histories used throughout this paper.  We retain that terminology and
formalism unchanged.

The division of labour is therefore
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \text{Paper 0: expansions, arithmetic motions, the models } \mathfrak E_0
  \text{ and } \mathfrak E_1,\\
  \text{ripple geometry, affine cocycles, ACS and tearing, contact
  structure},\\
  \text{Paper I: intrinsic sequential histories, bilateral projective
  evaluation, the Hecke sublanguage, zero geometry}.
\end{gathered}
\]
This is the reverse of the arrangement that held before the amendment recorded
in \texttt{governance/00b-paper-0-geometric-foundation-amendment.md}: the
geometric chapters that formerly stood here are now developed there, at the same
labels and with the same proofs.  Paper~I repeats exactly one imported
definition --- the regular AES and its canonical frame, in
Section~\ref{sec:regular-aes-interface} --- because the zero theorems below are
stated in those terms; every other result of Paper~0 is cited rather than
repeated.

Two conventions are shared and used identically in both papers.  A pole or
projective value at infinity never certifies ordinary admissibility of a
division step; that distinction remains active here.  And a historical word such
as ``left-expanded'' is meaningful only under the explicit operand-slot
recursion fixed in Paper~0.
