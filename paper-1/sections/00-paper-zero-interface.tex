Paper~0 supplies the elementary pure-comb prelude used here.  It fixes explicit
left- and right-expanded conventions, draws affine prefix evaluation as a point
path in the zeroth-kind upper-half-plane model, and draws projective condition
pullback as a ripple pencil tangent at the current pole.  It also treats
reciprocal expansion, the projective point at infinity, affine and reciprocal
fixed points, and the common $2\times2$ matrix representation.

The present paper begins after that visual and elementary layer.  Its first
problem is intrinsic: which finite binary expression trees have a unique legal
order of internal evaluation, including mixed operand-slot patterns?  The
answer is the single-spine classification.  Choosing an accumulator then gives
the marked spinal histories used throughout this paper.  We retain that
terminology and formalism unchanged.

The division of labor is therefore
\[
\begin{gathered}
  \text{Paper 0: pure left/right combs, path/ripple geometry,
  reciprocal and pole calculus},\\
  \text{Paper I: intrinsic sequential histories, bilateral projective
  evaluation, affine flow, torsion, and contact curvature}.
\end{gathered}
\]
Paper~I repeats the matrix representatives and the projective-generation theorem
needed for logical self-containment, but Paper~0 is the canonical source for
the elementary path/ripple exposition.  In particular, a pole or projective
value at infinity never certifies ordinary admissibility of a division step;
that distinction remains active in both papers.
