% Canonical Paper I source.

This section collects the classical theorems of one-variable calculus and their
realizations in the three layers developed above: the bulk (the contact model
$\mathcal C=\mathbb R^3_{(u,v,a)}$ of Section~\ref{sec:differential-theory}), the
surface (the two \emph{Legendre quotients} of $\mathcal C$ --- the charge plane
$\pi(u,v,a)=(u,v)$ and the Reeb quotient
$Q(u,v,a)=(-ae^{-\lambda v},e^{-\lambda v})$ of Paper~0
(\texttt{eq:p0-contact-quotient-map}, \texttt{thm:legendre-dual-tearing})), and
the series (the free Lie algebra of Section~\ref{sec:series-theory}).  Each
entry is a dictionary row, not a new theorem: the statements are proved in the
sections cited, and a row only records the correspondence, with its status.

Throughout, $\gamma,\delta$ are marked histories, $X_i$ their infinitesimal
generators, $\Omega(\gamma)$ the Magnus series, $\alpha$ the contact form, and
$\beta=-\lambda a\,dv$ the boundary potential of the Stokes reading.
The surface column is the pair $(\pi,Q)$: the $\pi$-side carries the weighted
area form $e^{M_*-M}\,dA\wedge dM$ on the accumulative commutative space
$\mathcal A=\mathbb R^2_{(A,M)}$ ($A=\mu u$, $M=\lambda v$), and the $Q$-side
carries the hyperbolic area form $d\mathrm{vol}_{g_{\mu,\lambda}}$ of the basic
model $\mathfrak E_0$.  The pants side-surface $S_G$ of
Section~\ref{sec:theory-of-caps} is a third, purely topological surface whose
holonomy realizes this column in one specific model, not the column itself
(Remark~\ref{rem:surface-is-legendre-quotient}).

\subsection{Reading the dictionary}

The four columns carry the same classical content at four levels.  The bulk and
series columns are the two sides of the contact model; the surface column is the
Legendre pair $(\pi,Q)$ through which the bulk descends.  The non-commutativity
of the bulk is what distinguishes every AEG entry from its classical form: the
classical ``difference'' $f(b)-f(a)$ becomes the order defect
$T_{\mathrm{open}}$, and the classical ``derivative'' $f'$ becomes the curvature
$\mu\lambda$.  Consequently the mean-value and Stokes rows are the two anchors
of the whole dictionary --- they express, respectively, that a defect is realized
at a mean point and that a bulk integral is realized on the surface, read dually
as a weighted area on the charge plane $\pi$ and a hyperbolic area on the Reeb
quotient $Q$.

\begin{remark}[The three surfaces: Legendre quotients versus pants]
\label{rem:surface-is-legendre-quotient}
The ``surface'' of this section is not the side surface $S_G$ of the thickened
body of Section~\ref{sec:theory-of-caps}.  Three surfaces are kept distinct.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Charge plane $\pi$:} the $\pi$-quotient $\pi(u,v,a)=(u,v)$, the
  accumulative commutative space $\mathcal A=\mathbb R^2_{(A,M)}$, carrying the
  exact Lagrangian form $e^{-M}dA$ and the weighted area
  $e^{M_*-M}dA\wedge dM$ ($\texttt{thm:torsion-stokes}$).
  \item \textbf{Hyperbolic model $Q$:} the Reeb quotient
  $Q(u,v,a)=(-ae^{-\lambda v},e^{-\lambda v})$, i.e.\ the basic model
  $\mathfrak E_0$, carrying the Hamiltonian form $a\,dM$ and the hyperbolic area
  form ($\texttt{prop:p0-contact-quotient}$,
  $\texttt{thm:legendre-dual-tearing}$).
  \item \textbf{Pants $S_G$:} the topological side surface of a thickened body
  (Section~\ref{sec:theory-of-caps}), which carries the surface potential
  $\beta=(-\lambda a\,dv)|_{S_G}$ only under the declared analytic thickening
  (Remark~\ref{rem:analytic-thickening-declared}); its seam and puncture
  holonomies are a specific realization of the same bulk--surface correspondence,
  not the correspondence itself.
\end{enumerate}
The first two are the conjugate Legendre pair of Paper~0
($\S9$, \texttt{subsec:legendre-duality}); the third is a downstream
application.
\end{remark}
