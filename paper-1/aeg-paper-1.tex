\begin{abstract}
Paper~0 develops the elementary pure-comb prelude: left-expanded point paths,
right-expanded ripple pencils, reciprocals, poles, infinity, fixed points, and
their common matrix representation.  The present paper begins with the
intrinsic history layer.  We classify finite binary expression trees having a
unique legal order of internal evaluation: their internal vertices form one
spine.  Marking an accumulator produces a chronological word of one-hole
contexts while keeping operand-slot chirality distinct from temporal reversal
and path inverse.  Nondegenerate bilateral contexts act projectively on
$\PP^1(K)$ and generate $\PGL_2(K)$; the contexts fixing infinity form the
affine Borel sector.  The positive real affine component yields the arithmetic
flow $da/ds=\mu\cos\theta+\lambda a\sin\theta$, a complete hyperbolic model,
regular-zero rigidity, affine cocycles, an exact weighted ACS torsion formula,
and the contact curvature $[D_u,D_v]=\mu\lambda\partial_a$.  Paper~I retains
the marked-history terminology and theorem-level foundations; Paper~0 is the
canonical elementary source for the path/ripple and reciprocal--pole--infinity
pictures.
\end{abstract}
