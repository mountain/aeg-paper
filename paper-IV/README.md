# Arithmetic Expression Geometry IV — Projective Condensation and Computational Complexity

This directory records the destination of projective quotient, condensation, and
complexity material outside Paper I.  It is not yet a completed manuscript.

## Imports from Paper I

- marked bilateral histories and ordinary/projective domain labels;
- the projective evaluation into `PGL_2(K)`;
- the affine/Borel sector and cocycle coordinates;
- the distinction between history, operator, charge, and endpoint equality.

## Migrated source families

- the quotient, bivaluation, projector, and condensation portions of
  `notes/bilateral_projective_condensation.tex`;
- relation/neutrality material not retained in Paper I Appendix E;
- `notes/rg_*`, resource-geometry notes, and complexity discussions.

## Status boundary

The rank-one projector and homogeneous quotient skeleton has a substantial algebraic
core.  A canonical notion of process residue, a numerical measure of lost information,
and any equivalence among representation, time, and space complexity remain research
hypotheses.  Noncommutativity or hyperbolic geometry alone implies no computational
lower bound.
