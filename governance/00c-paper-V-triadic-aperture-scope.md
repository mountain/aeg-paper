## 1. Why a fifth paper exists

Papers 0--IV develop one process-to-result architecture: expansion and motion
(Paper 0), sequential histories and projective semantics (Paper I), hyperbolic real
function theory (Paper II), singular zero geometry and tubes (Paper III), and
projective condensation with computational cost (Paper IV).

The research corpus `adva/docs/research`, notes `0001`--`0127`, develops a second,
largely independent body of mathematics that none of Papers 0--IV owns: a theory of
**three coordinated typed readings of one carrier**, of the **observation fibres and
residuals** those readings produce, and of the **syntax of apertures** (typed holes)
through which such readings are filled and checked. That body of work has reached
enough internal coherence to be stated as a paper rather than as a note series.

Paper V is that paper. It is a **two-part monograph**:

* **Part I — Triadic characteristic geometry.**
* **Part II — Threaded apertures and the six-port kernel.**

The user-level decision of 2026-10-20 is recorded here as the authorizing instruction
for this scope: absorb the mathematics of notes `0001`--`0127`, and drop the
engineering background (see §6).

## 2. What Paper V owns

Paper V may define and develop the following as its own main theory.

### 2.1 Observation, fibres, and characteristics

* the finite observer contract and its components (observation map, equivalence,
  projectors, budget);
* observation fibres, characteristic as an operational quotient, and the
  certificate-plus-residual requirement;
* the general non-faithfulness pattern: extensional shadows (values, gradients,
  spectra, terminal cycles, frontier snapshots, objectified surreal numbers,
  energies) do not recover construction history, stated as explicit no-go results
  rather than as prohibitions.

### 2.2 Three-aspect and chirality structure

* the quadratic scalar family $A_\kappa=\mathbb R[j]/(j^2-\kappa)$ and the
  negative-inverse involution criterion;
* the distinction between the projective involution $[J]^2=[I]$ and its oriented
  lift $J^2=-I$, and the definition of chirality as the retained central sign;
* the six-state carrier $V\oplus\Lambda^2 V$, including the orientation expression
  $\Omega$, the sign trichotomy $\Omega^2=-\varepsilon_T\varepsilon_S\varepsilon_R$,
  and the filtration induced by a designated causal line;
* the derivation, on a declared finite cut interval, of the Clifford anticommutator
  from cut-membership cochains together with oriented interchange.

### 2.3 Triadic characteristic inference

* the three typed interpretations and their variance signature $(-,+)$ pattern;
* inference as constrained intersection of typed observation fibres;
* the deformed crossing laws and the word-rewrite / typed-DAG boundary;
* the typed multiplicity profile (algebraic degree, real fibre cardinality, sheet
  count, occurrence multiplicity) and the proof that these are independent;
* invariance as the trivial case of equivariance, and the binomial coaction as the
  reason translation cannot cross a power inside a word normal form.

### 2.4 Crossings, through-forms, and monodromy

* the through-crossing cospan $F_-\to F_0\leftarrow F_+$ and its resolution torsor;
* monodromy as the difference of two through-resolutions, and the explicit
  derivation $M_{\mathrm{Legendre}}=T_\delta^2$ from the quadratic base change;
* cellular realizations of Picard--Lefschetz transvection;
* the through form as a fibre product over a typed singular middle object, including
  the independence of number-order and through-compatibility.

### 2.5 Logics generated by a satisfaction form

* Sierpinski-valued satisfaction forms over typed carriers, their opposite-pair
  sections, and the entailment preorder;
* the standing constraint that sections, not the ambient powerset, generate
  admissible propositions;
* observer transport with adjoint quantifiers;
* directional-energy enrichment and its exact coarse-plus-residual decomposition.

### 2.6 Apertures and threaded derivability (Part II)

* the typed hole (aperture) as a primitive whose open and close operations are not
  inverses;
* finite terminal-world carriers, filling fibres, and finite adequacy of threaded
  propositional and predicate logic under explicit hypotheses;
* the ordered linear natural-deduction fragment, its soundness, its incompleteness,
  its grade invariant, strong normalization, local and global confluence, the
  subformula property, and decidable derivability;
* the six-port whole-cut kernel: the counting theorem $n\ge 2m$ hence $n_{\min}=6$
  under its stated contract, the carrier, the cell--carrier--view spine, and the
  $M_6$ boundary bridge from two fixed-point-free involutions.

## 3. What Paper V must not develop

Paper V inherits, and must not weaken, the prohibitions of `00`, `00a`, `00b`, `01`.

It must not restate as its own main theory:

* the geometric construction of arithmetic expression spaces, their motions,
  commutativization, or contact structure (Paper 0 owns them);
* intrinsic dependency-poset tree classification, marked spinal histories,
  operand-slot chirality as a syntactic primitive, the projective quotient tower, or
  regular-zero rigidity (Paper I);
* arithmetic holomorphic function theory, boundary-value theory, or spectral theory
  of horizontal operators (Paper II);
* singular zero-locus classification, zero networks, tubes, braids, or knot
  invariants (Paper III);
* projective condensation, contextual-residual state lower bounds, or
  computational-complexity conclusions (Paper IV).

Where Paper V needs one of those objects it must import it by citation to the owning
paper and must not silently redefine it.

Paper V must also not:

* treat a calibration, an exhaustive fixture check, or an executed program as a
  proof of a general statement;
* promote a proposal to a definition with ontological force without saying so;
* claim that a finite experiment demonstrates universality, non-computability, or a
  physical realization;
* claim that the three named readings are canonically identified merely because
  three names are available — triadicity is a property to be certified, not a
  labelling convention (§5.4);
* infer order (a total $L\mid R$ comparison) from an energy, a grade, or a scalar.

## 5. Claim status and honesty requirements

### 5.1 Status labels

Every substantial result carries one of the labels fixed in `AGENTS.md`. In practice
Paper V will use, and must use honestly:

* `proved` / `proved with stated hypotheses` — only where the paper contains the
  argument;
* `calibration` — established only by finite exhaustive check of a declared fixture;
* `proposal` — a definitional or organizational choice with no theorem attached;
* `conjecture` / `open problem` — explicitly unproved;
* `no-go` — a proved negative result, which is a theorem and must be proved as one.

### 5.2 Two-standard rule

A statement of the form "no observer of class $\mathcal O$ can determine $X$ from
$Y$" is a theorem only when the class of observations and the sense of determination
are both fixed. Otherwise it is a calibration or a proposal.

### 5.3 Residual duty

Every quotient result must carry either (a) an exact reconstruction statement, or
(b) a named residual whose contents are characterized. "The quotient loses nothing
important" is not an admissible claim in this paper.

### 5.4 Triadicity is certified, not named

Three readings of one carrier count as a triadic structure only when an explicit
coherence datum is supplied: the pairwise crossings must be given, and the residual
of the cycle must be exhibited. The corpus's own finite Legendre prism fails to
produce an irreducible triadic defect; that failure is reported in Paper V as the
paper's principal negative calibration, not concealed.

## 6. Treatment of the source corpus

The source of Paper V is `adva/docs/research`, notes `0001`--`0127` (133 files
including sub-numbered variants; surveyed 2026-10-20 into ten digests at
`paper-5/sources/survey/`).

Admission rules:

1. **Mathematics admitted, engineering excluded.** Checked-program harnesses, test
   suites, Rust/Python authority splits, work-package phases, document formats,
   ledgers, CI gates, digests, CLI flags, and "not a stable API" governance
   boilerplate are excluded. Only the mathematical content is carried forward.
2. **Statuses are inherited, not upgraded.** A note's own `Status:` line bounds what
   Paper V may claim. Where a note says "bounded calibration", Paper V must not say
   "theorem".
3. **Fifteen files are predominantly engineering.** `0026`, `0050` (LABS), `0054`
   (sorting networks), `0074`, `0090`, `0092`, the `0102` follow-up trio, `0108`,
   `0110`, `0115`, `0116`, `0123`, and one SVG figure contribute no main-text
   mathematics and are cited at most as provenance.
4. **Superseded formulations are not silently resurrected.** Where a later note
   corrects an earlier one (for example the correction that the forward
   $\mathrm{NF}/\mathrm{NF}$ duality law of `0066` is false, or that raw cut
   frontiers are not multi-affine in `0007`), Paper V states the corrected form.
5. **Provenance is recorded.** `paper-5/appendices/app-D-claim-ledger.tex` maps every
   paper-level result to its source note number(s) and status.
